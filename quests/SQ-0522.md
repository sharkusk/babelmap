--- note 2026-07-29T04:19:15Z ---

SCOPE DECIDED (user, 2026-07-29). Queue position 1 of 3: SQ-0522 -> SQ-0525 -> SQ-0391.
Collapse goes first because it deletes whole connectors, which changes how many crossings
are left for SQ-0525 to restyle.

DECISION: "Everything per pair." Every passage between the same two rooms draws as ONE
line, whatever its direction — compass AND Up/Down/In/Out. The retained pairing prefers a
true opposite (a straight line); every other passage becomes a direction icon stacked at
its own end. This replaces today's merge-stub T-junctions as well.

WHERE IT IS TODAY. `route::select_shared_paths` (SQ-0225) already collapses same-pair
compass edges into a retained connector plus `secondary_exit`/`secondary_entry` records,
which `render/map.rs:1513` stamps as arrow glyphs in the border slot beside the retained
arrowhead. It is gated on `fwd.len() >= 2 && bwd.len() >= 2` — "collapse ONLY true
bidirectional redundancy". Dropping that guard is the core of this quest.

THE CASE IT MISSES, and the one to test with. Adventure's maze pairs are 1-out/1-back and
NON-RECIPROCAL: `65169 -S-> 53329` with `53329 -W-> 65169`. fwd=1, bwd=1, so no collapse;
and the router's dedupe only skips an edge whose exact reciprocal-opposite was emitted, so
BOTH are drawn — two separate connectors between the same two rooms, crossing each other.
That is the rat's nest the user is looking at. `~/.babelmap/saves/advent.blb.save/default.babelmap`
is the fixture; its maze is 12 rooms with many such pairs.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES / TRAPS.
- Up/Down currently gets its OWN trunk on purpose: `ch_key = (pair.0, pair.1, is_up_down)`
  (SQ-0224) keeps a pair's vertical and horizontal passages independent so neither
  collapses into the other as a merge stub. "Everything per pair" means retiring that
  channel split. Check what SQ-0224's tests lock in before removing it.
- In/Out/Unknown carry NO layout offset and are not routed at all — they are portal badges
  anchored at the room. Folding them into a pair's line only works when the pair already
  HAS a line. Suggested rule: an In/Out/Unknown edge whose pair carries a drawn connector
  joins it as a stacked icon; one whose pair has no connector keeps its badge as today.
- `select_shared_paths`'s `pick` already prefers `edge_is_satisfied` then the opposite
  direction; the user's "stack arrows next to the reciprocal direction if possible,
  otherwise minimize path crossings" is that preference plus a crossing-count tiebreak when
  no opposite exists. `render_stats`/`count_overlaps` in render/map.rs gives the crossing
  count to score against.
- Existing tests lock in the merge-stub rendering for one-sided redundancy (the #33/#175
  shape named in the code comment). They will need revising, not deleting — capture what
  they were protecting.

--- note 2026-07-29T06:41:09Z ---

ATTEMPTED AND REVERTED (2026-07-29). Implemented in ebf194a6, backed out in 84667762
after the user saw it in play: too many direction icons stacked on a room's border to
stay readable. REOPENED — the routing half worked, the display half did not.

WHAT WAS BUILT (recoverable from ebf194a6 if this is retried)
- `select_shared_paths` widened from `grid_offset` to `layout_offset` and from
  "collapse only true bidirectional redundancy (2+ each way)" to every pair. Retains at
  most one edge per direction-of-travel bucket; a compass passage always beats a
  staircase for the line; a back-edge is kept only where `back_edge_idx` will actually
  pair it (an Up/Down edge pairs solely with its exact opposite).
- Secondary attachment falls back to a pure-Up/Down connector when the pair has no
  compass one.
- Renderer: `arrow_for_direction` extended to the portal directions from the themeable
  `PortalGlyphs`; portal secondaries styled `map.connector_portal`; they no longer drop
  out of the diagonal-end branch on a missing grid offset.
- `draw_deduped_updown_border_glyphs` / `updown_pair_deduped` deleted — dormant since
  SQ-0224 reversed SQ-0219, revived by the change, then redundant.

WHY IT FAILED — a display constraint, not a routing one.
A room box is BOX_W=11 x BOX_H=5. A side's usable border cells are its interior extent
(3 on a vertical side, 9 on a horizontal one) and the retained connector's ARROWHEAD
already owns one. Past two collapsed directions at a single end there is nowhere left
to put a marker that is not a corner, another connector's anchor, or an occupied cell.
I hit two separate versions of this while building it:
  1. marching one direction and clamping stacked two markers on the SAME cell, the
     second silently erasing the first;
  2. where the anchor already sat at the end of a short vertical side, the clamp landed
     ON the arrowhead and erased it (#65169 visibly lost its right-side arrow).
Picking from the free cells nearest the arrowhead fixed both, but only pushes the wall
back — it cannot manufacture cells. Adventure's maze, the map this was meant to fix, is
exactly where pairs carry the most directions.

THE ROUTING WIN WAS REAL, measured on ~/.babelmap/saves/advent.blb.save/default.babelmap:
57 -> 51 connectors, 168 -> 122 crossings (-27%), illegal overlaps 0 before and after.
So the collapse is worth keeping IF the icons get somewhere to live. Options to weigh
before retrying, none of them chosen yet:
  - a compact multi-direction glyph, so N+S+Up is ONE cell rather than three;
  - a count badge ("x3") that defers the detail to the room inspector;
  - collapse only up to N directions and leave the rest as lines;
  - widen the box, or give the icons a gutter cell outside the border;
  - collapse in the ROUTER (for the crossing win) but keep drawing the extra
    passages' arrowheads only, without full lines.
Whichever way it goes, decide the icon presentation FIRST — the router change is the
easy half and lands cleanly once there is somewhere to put the result.

ALSO RESTORED BY THE REVERT: SQ-0224's separate compass/Up-Down trunks, SQ-0225's
2+-each-way guard, the merge stubs, and the ten tests that pin them. SQ-0356's
chain-merge fixture is back to its original single-pair form.

CORRECTION to this quest's earlier note: the claim that Adventure's non-reciprocal
maze pairs (S out, W back) drew two crossing lines was WRONG — `back_edge_idx` already
pairs those. The real source of the doubled lines was SQ-0224's channel split giving a
pair's compass and Up/Down passages separate trunks. Five maze pairs drew a solid line
beside a dotted staircase, weaving through each other.
