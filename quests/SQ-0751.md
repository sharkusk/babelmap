MEASURED by SQ-0748's agent, reported as found-not-fixed.

`finish_command_turn` marks the screen-clear boundary at the START of a turn, regardless of where within that turn the erase actually falls. A turn that prints and THEN erases would therefore keep its pre-erase text on screen — the clear is recorded as having happened before output that in fact preceded it.

Latent today: no game in the agent's sweep does that. Beyond Zork's prologue turn erases then prints, which top-anchors correctly; only the v6 `ScreenClear` element can split a turn at all. The sweep covered Zork I and Enchanter (v3), Bureaucracy and Trinity (v4), Border Zone (v5), Beyond Zork, LostPig (v8), Zork Zero / Arthur / Journey / Shogun (v6), Counterfeit Monkey and Kerkerkruip (Glulx).

Left alone deliberately rather than widened mid-lane. Whoever takes it needs a game that actually prints-then-erases in one turn, or a synthetic story that does — without one, any fix is unfalsifiable, and this codebase's rule is that a fix must be shown to fail with the original symptom when reverted.

Related: SQ-0748, which fixed the adjacent off-by-one (an anchor AT the end of the transcript read as "no anchor" instead of "cleared screen with nothing printed into it").
