## The one fact

**The hybrid ring is defined as the complement of the story box.** There is no flank object at all — a flank is whatever lands in the third or fourth rect of `pane − viewport`, so its vertical extent is the story window's by definition. The story box is fixed FIRST, from the game's declared win0 rect, and everything else is derived from it.

Nearly every patch on this path is downstream of that. From §4's judgement column:

| mechanism | line | what it costs |
|---|---|---|
| `picture_takeover`'s **four** OR'd hatches | `screen.rs:3942-3953` | all four say *"there are pixels here the ring has nowhere to put"*. A content-built ring needs one |
| the SQ-0747 remainder walk | `screen.rs:1246-1329` | **60 lines and four measured pane sizes to give one row of a flank column back to the flank** |
| the Extend clip family | `screen.rs:1059-1146` | four patches, including one the document calls "a patch on a patch" (`:1135-1138`) |
| `overlay_strip` viewport push | `screen.rs:982-1014` | *"step (b) done by hand, for one case"* |
| the flank seam | §5 | MEASURED on Zork Zero at 100x40: rows 1–6 tile at magnification 1.2250/1.2250 off the **raw** canvas, rows 7–37 at 1.2308/1.2237 off the **extended** one — half a pixel of shear. Invisible today only because both pieces read continuous artwork |

And a second tier that dissolves as a consequence: `has_menu` (`:709-735`, narrowed twice in two quests — §4 calls that "the signature of a proxy"; what it is really asking is *"can the ring lay this screen out?"* and it answers by pattern-matching a screen shape), its undocumented `STATUS_BAND_ROWS = 4` (`:3510`), `menu_over_art` (`:757-762`, SQ-0886), and `frozen_head` (`session.rs:1667-1668`, SQ-0890 — which exists ONLY because `menu_over_art` routes that frame to the composite).

## Also in scope, because they are the same class

- **"Is there art here?" has two implementations with different rounding**: `strip_has_art` (`screen.rs:1181-1195`) inverts device→native with `as u32` truncation on both axes; `over_art` (`screen.rs:5084-5089`) works in exact native coordinates via `region_has_opaque`. They are asked about overlapping regions on the same frame and can disagree by one native pixel at a boundary — the ceil-vs-round class CLAUDE.md names as the usual cause of v6 geometry defects. Unify them.
- **`recognize` is called on three different column ranges for one flank** — `screen.rs:1136` (band rect), `:1533` (twice-mutated strip rect), `v6_layout.rs:4428` (story-box-derived, raster). One flank, one classification.

## The ordering this enables

The user's model, and the reason this quest exists: (a) render the side panels with tiling, as one continuous column at one scale over the full pane height; (b) determine the valid text region from what the panels leave; (c) communicate that region to the story.

- (a) is precluded today — no flank object exists.
- (b) is **written and dead**: `story_viewport` (`v6_layout.rs:1448`) is exactly shrink-until-clear-then-quantize with no production caller. §6 notes it "would need its oracle canvas fixed". **SQ-0893 may remove it; if so its body records what it was and the commit that carried it, so recover it from there rather than rewriting.**
- (c) is available and **deliberately disabled for v6 at five sites**. We report a fixed 640x400 / 80x25 derived from the art, and `$30` is `chars × 8` with no reference to any window or margin. **The app already does this narrowing for v1–5, 7 and 8** (`screen.rs:440-461`) — every version except the one that needs it. Note the standing constraint at `session.rs:2733-2742`: the app deliberately does not clip what `get_wind_prop` reports today, so changing (c) is a decision with its own history — read it before touching it, and treat (c) as a separate step that may not belong in this quest at all.

## The rule, and why it has five clauses

A flank owns every contiguous row it may own — art or bare ground, IN ITS OWN COLUMNS — stopping at chrome text, at a blank row bridged between two text rows (SQ-0508), at a prose panel, and at the menu band. Four of those five clauses were added because a capture produced a defect, not because they seemed prudent:

- **The axis-inversion in the scouting note is wrong** and I nearly shipped it. It tiles exactly on all eight frames. Arthur's status bar is a full-width `strip:text(72 runs)` and a full-height flank cuts that single row three ways.
- Without the menu clause: Journey's `menu:text(22 runs)` came back **59x6 at (38,32)** instead of 98x6, its left 37 columns lost to flank art.
- Without the own-columns art test: Journey's Amiga press at 163x61 grew a **third `flank-divider`** — band ink at col 162 beside the real `│` at col 163, the doubled rule its own suite forbids.
- Without SQ-0508's bridge: Shogun's status lines stopped being adjacent, "Moves" moving from row 1 to row 2 with frame art across the row between.

Final corpus, matching the scouting prediction exactly: Zork Zero 1..37 continuous; Arthur 2..13 + 15..37 split AT HIS STATUS BAR; Shogun (3..37) and Journey (1..31) unmoved, both measured as already optimal — **the axis-inversion would have broken both**.

`ChromeStrip::Art` carries its role too; ten downstream stages had the same width heuristic, which is what produced Journey's third divider and made two suites count Arthur's 90x13 top band as a fifth flank.

The row classifier moved out of `decompose_chrome_strips` into `ChromeRowOracle`, shared by the ring construction and the decomposition, with `region_has_art` joining it — §6's "two places deciding the same thing by different rules" not made worse.

## The user's proposal: transparent story viewport, art showing through

Sound, and the mechanism is real. VERIFIED against the kitty graphics protocol spec (fetched, not recalled):

- "Negative z-index values mean that the images will be drawn under the text."
- "Negative z-index values below INT32_MIN/2 (-1,073,741,824) will be drawn under cells with non-default background colors."

Three layers: `z >= 0` above text; `-1073741824 < z < 0` below text but ABOVE cell backgrounds; `z <= -1073741824` below both. So art behind story text needs a negative `z` AND the story cells carrying a default background — which is exactly what "transparent viewport" means.

Two obstacles, one fatal to it as a general answer:

1. **We emit VIRTUAL placements.** We hand-author the escape (`graphics.rs:1901`, `…a=T,U=1,f=32,…`) so adding `z=` is trivial — but `U=1` means the pixels appear in cells holding the placeholder character, and those cells cannot also carry story text. This needs classic placements with absolute positioning, whose lifetime/scroll semantics differ (and SQ-0772 records why the virtual path was chosen).
2. **It is KITTY-ONLY.** Half-blocks ARE glyphs — the image is `▀` cells with fg/bg, so nothing can be drawn over it. Sixel has no z-order. On either backend the only portable way to get text over art is to rasterise the text INTO the image.

## CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ACCEPTANCE CRITERION — restated, and stronger than the earlier answer

Earlier in this quest I answered "raster is not removable" on STRUCTURAL grounds (the ring is bounded by the viewport). True, but secondary. **The binding reason is protocol-level:** any frame needing text over art has no portable expression except rasterising the text, so the composite must exist for sixel and half-block terminals however good the ring becomes.

The most the ring can achieve is to stop NEEDING the composite on kitty. That reframes the work: a transparent-viewport + negative-z path would be a kitty-only fast path ALONGSIDE a composite that stays. Worth knowing before anyone invests in it as "the change that removes raster".
