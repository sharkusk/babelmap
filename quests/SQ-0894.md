## The one fact

**The hybrid ring is defined as the complement of the story box.** There is no flank object at all — a flank is whatever lands in the third or fourth rect of `pane − viewport`, so its vertical extent is the story window's by definition. The story box is fixed FIRST, from the game's declared win0 rect, and everything else is derived from it.

Nearly every patch on this path is downstream of that. From §4's judgement column:

And a second tier that dissolves as a consequence: `has_menu` (`:709-735`, narrowed twice in two quests — §4 calls that "the signature of a proxy"; what it is really asking is *"can the ring lay this screen out?"* and it answers by pattern-matching a screen shape), its undocumented `STATUS_BAND_ROWS = 4` (`:3510`), `menu_over_art` (`:757-762`, SQ-0886), and `frozen_head` (`session.rs:1667-1668`, SQ-0890 — which exists ONLY because `menu_over_art` routes that frame to the composite).

## Also in scope, because they are the same class

- **"Is there art here?" has two implementations with different rounding**: `strip_has_art` (`screen.rs:1181-1195`) inverts device→native with `as u32` truncation on both axes; `over_art` (`screen.rs:5084-5089`) works in exact native coordinates via `region_has_opaque`. They are asked about overlapping regions on the same frame and can disagree by one native pixel at a boundary — the ceil-vs-round class CLAUDE.md names as the usual cause of v6 geometry defects. Unify them.
- **`recognize` is called on three different column ranges for one flank** — `screen.rs:1136` (band rect), `:1533` (twice-mutated strip rect), `v6_layout.rs:4428` (story-box-derived, raster). One flank, one classification.

## The ordering this enables

The user's model, and the reason this quest exists: (a) render the side panels with tiling, as one continuous column at one scale over the full pane height; (b) determine the valid text region from what the panels leave; (c) communicate that region to the story.

- (a) is precluded today — no flank object exists.
- (b) is **written and dead**: `story_viewport` (`v6_layout.rs:1448`) is exactly shrink-until-clear-then-quantize with no production caller. §6 notes it "would need its oracle canvas fixed". **SQ-0893 may remove it; if so its body records what it was and the commit that carried it, so recover it from there rather than rewriting.**
- (c) is available and **deliberately disabled for v6 at five sites**. We report a fixed 640x400 / 80x25 derived from the art, and `$30` is `chars × 8` with no reference to any window or margin. **The app already does this narrowing for v1–5, 7 and 8** (`screen.rs:440-461`) — every version except the one that needs it. Note the standing constraint at `session.rs:2733-2742`: the app deliberately does not clip what `get_wind_prop` reports today, so changing (c) is a decision with its own history — read it before touching it, and treat (c) as a separate step that may not belong in this quest at all.

## How to do this without repeating the last attempt

SQ-0892's first lane was stopped for patching symptoms. The failure mode to avoid is fixing each reported defect where it surfaces. **Change the structure, then delete what the structure makes unnecessary, and let the corpus prove it.**

- **Measure before deleting, and say the measurement.** The precedent is SQ-0888's lane: it deleted `story_text_box` only after measuring it answered `None` on every corpus frame at boot and on every turn. A mechanism that is still load-bearing should be KEPT with a stated reason — a truthful "still needed because X" is a good outcome.
- **Do not delete tests to make a removal pass.** Retarget them, or state plainly why they no longer describe anything.
- Every item in the tables above is a hypothesis about what will dissolve, not an instruction to remove it. If `menu_over_art` still earns its place after the restructure, keep it and say why.

## Testing — read this before writing a single assertion

- **The flagship Shogun render suite pins the HALFBLOCKS backend**, `crates/app/tests/suites/v6_shogun_menu_ground.rs:118` with its reason at `:116-117`. It therefore **structurally cannot see a kitty placement defect** — and SQ-0890 recorded that this suite passed while the credit collision was on screen. Placement assertions must go through `crates/app/tests/pty_stream/`, not this suite.
- `hybrid_draws_the_frame_raster_already_drew` (`:219`) asserts hybrid/raster **parity**, so it cannot catch a defect both modes share. Named as a check on hybrid; is actually a check on agreement.
- The corpus of merged fixes on this path is the real gate: SQ-0505/0511/0571/0582/0585/0587/0698/0725/0739/0747/0750/0779/0818/0819/0830/0881/0886/0888/0890 all have suites. They must stay green, and where one moves you must say why the new behaviour is correct.
