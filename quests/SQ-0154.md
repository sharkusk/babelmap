ROOT CAUSE (reproduced by driving beyondzork-r57 with interpreter number 1 and answering YES to "Is this a VT220?"): the VT220 title bar frames the centered room name with half-block bars — `▐  Hilltop  ▌` (▐=U+2590 in col 1, ▌=U+258C). RESOLUTION: added `deframe()` — maps box-drawing/block glyphs (U+2500–U+259F) to spaces for PARSING only (never display); `▐ Hilltop ▌` → centered "Hilltop", accepted only under PlayerParent validation (obj 48).
