**Two of the three presses are affected**, and the collision is verbatim:

| press | fixture | bottom-left of the credits screen |
|---|---|---|
| Amiga r295/s890321 | `James Clavell's Shogun.adf` | `Copyright (c) 1988 by InfocomQUIT the game` |

| ProDOS r311/s890510 | `shogun_s1.dsk` | **clean** — "You may choose to:" and the menu sit in a wider box with room |

On the two affected presses the first three credit lines (`SHOGUN` / `A Story of Japan` / `Copyright (c) 1988 by Infocom`) are painted a SECOND time at the bottom left, in the game's own four-row prose box, running straight into the menu column so that a credit line and a menu item share a row and abut with no gap. A `[more]` sits at the bottom right. The credits are already drawn correctly in the canvas above, so this second rendition is pure duplication — it adds nothing and destroys the menu's legibility on the game's first screen.

WHERE THIS CAME FROM, and it is not a defect in SQ-0886's premise. That fix is right: a chrome graphics window carrying opaque pixels has no ring to live in, so the frame must take the composite. The consequence is that hybrid's prose viewport on this screen is now Shogun's own four-row box rather than the whole pane — which is what `v6_render = "raster"` has always shipped here, and the SQ-0886 lane flagged it honestly as "the consequence worth your eye" (its `more_pager_first_new_row` case moved for exactly this reason). What the lane's description understated is the SEVERITY: it reported the effect as "`[more]` engages on the boot turn and 'You may choose to:' is one page down", which reads as a pagination nicety. The captures show overlapping text that reads as garbage. Pagination is not the problem; the duplicate replay landing on top of the menu is.

TWO CANDIDATE SHAPES — measure before choosing, do not assume the smaller one:
  1. **Suppress the replay.** In the composite, when the frame is a menu takeover and the story box is this small, the transcript should not re-emit lines the canvas has already drawn. Attacks the duplication at its source and is probably the honest fix, since the second rendition carries no information.
  2. **Clip the composite's transcript** to the story window's own native rect so it cannot paint into the menu's columns whatever it contains. Narrower, and it leaves the duplication in place — merely tidier.

Option 1 is the one to try first, but confirm by measurement that nothing on this frame is transcript-only before suppressing anything: if any line reaches the user ONLY via the replay, suppressing it loses text, and that is worse than the collision.

REGRESSION GUARD THIS NEEDS: the existing SQ-0886 suite `v6_shogun_menu_ground.rs` asserts the artwork reaches the pane and that hybrid matches raster cell-for-cell. It passed while this collision was on screen, so cell-vs-raster equality does NOT catch it — raster has the same duplication. A new case must assert the property directly: **no row on this frame carries both a credit fragment and a menu item**, on all three presses, both `honor_game_colours` modes. Fixtures gitignored — skip vacuously.
