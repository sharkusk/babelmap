SURVEY of the user's actual images, measured 2026-08-13 by parsing the FAT12 BPB and root directory directly. Do not re-derive; verify if the corpus changes.

All of them are plain FAT12: 512-byte sectors, 2 sectors/cluster, 1 reserved, 2 FATs, 112 root entries, 3 sectors/FAT. 720K images are 1440 sectors, 360K are 720. **So the filesystem work here is trivial** — far simpler than the AmigaDOS parsing `blorb/src/adf.rs` already does by hand at zero dependency cost. That is NOT where the difficulty of this quest lives.

## Where the difficulty actually lives: one game's files span several images

**Zork Zero, 720K PC release (r393, serial 890714):**
* Disk 1 (vol `ZORK0 1`) — `ZORK0.ZIP` 300032 (the STORY; `.ZIP` here is Infocom's Z-machine Interpreter Program data, NOT PKZIP), `ZORK0.MG1` 226436 (MCGA art), `ZORKZERO.EXE`, `EZR.EXE`, `INSTALL.EXE`, `IZORK03.RUN`
* Disk 2 (vol `ZORK0 2`) — `ZORK0.CG1` 244507, and nothing else

**The Lost Treasures of Infocom, 5×720K (vol labels `Tresure.1` … `Tresure.5`):**
* floppy1 — `ZORK1.DAT`, `ZORK3.DAT`, `BEYONDZO.DAT` (262190, so v5), `INFIDEL.DAT`, plus `ZIP1.COM`/`ZIP2.COM`/`ZIP3.EXE`/`ZIP4.EXE`, `INFOCOM.EXE`, `SETUP.EXE`, `INSTALL.BAT`, `ANSI3/4/5.SYS`
* floppy2 — `HITCHHIK`, `ZORK2`, `ENCHANTE`, `BALLYHOO`, `SORCERER`, `SUSPECT`
* floppy3 — `LURKING`, `MOONMIST`, `STARCROS`, `SPELLBRE`, `DEADLINE`, `PLANETFA`
* floppy4 — `STATIONF`, `SUSPEND`, `WITNESS`, **`ZORK0.CG1`**, `ZORKZERO.EXE`
* floppy5 — **`ZORK0.EG1`** 333654, **`ZORK0.ZIP`** 300032

TWO STRUCTURAL FACTS THAT SHAPE THE DESIGN:

1. **A single game's story and its art live on DIFFERENT images.** Zork Zero's story is on collection disk 5 while its CGA art is on disk 4 and its EGA art is on disk 5; on the standalone 720K release the story and MCGA art are on disk 1 and the CGA art on disk 2. So mounting ONE image cannot in general produce a runnable game. This needs a **set** model — several images mounted together — not a per-file mount. The same question applies to the Amiga side, where Zork Zero also shipped on two disks (`adf.rs` says it was verified against both).
2. **A collection image holds MANY stories**, so the Amiga model of "find *the* story by content" does not transfer. `looks_like_story` would return 4 candidates on floppy1 and 6 on floppy2. This needs a LISTING and a choice — which is the picker's job, not the mounter's.

Art extension → video card is already understood in the codebase: `interpreter.rs` documents `.MG1` (MCGA), `.EG1`/`.EG2` (EGA) and `.CG1` (CGA) as `Flavour::Pc` — three cards, one machine. So a set that carries several art archives is a CHOICE of card, not of platform, and the existing `PictureOverride::flavour` path is where that choice already lives.

Volume labels look usable as set identity (`Tresure.1`…`Tresure.5`, `ZORK0 1`/`ZORK0 2`) but that is an observation from five samples, not a verified convention — do not build on it without checking more media.

Fixture note: these live in the user's gitignored `stories/`, so any test over them must skip vacuously when absent, and each release must be pinned in `crates/app/tests/suites/real_media_releases.rs`. The 360K and 720K Zork Zero images are both r393/890714, but per the project's standing rule a disk image is a different release from the bare story file — name the exact image in any finding.
