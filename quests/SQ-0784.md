    row of the panel titles :  "Denari"  ......fill......  "Primiera"
    upper score row         :  "9"       ......fill......  "81"
    lower score row         :  "1"       ......fill......  "71"

WHY THAT MATTERS FOR THE DIAGNOSIS: it is a BRIDGE BETWEEN TWO RUNS on one row, not a panel padded out to its window width. So the prime suspect is not `fill_explicit_bg_rows`'s bar padding but the rule-collapse fill described on SQ-0742 — *"fills from the previous drawn run's end to the next run's start so corners and the JOURNEY title don't punch holes"*. That behaviour exists precisely to bridge gaps between runs, and here it bridges gaps the game intended to leave open.

Note the tension it must be fixed against: on Journey that same bridging is WANTED (it stops the frame's corners and the `JOURNEY` title punching holes in the top rule), and SQ-0508 explicitly asked for "the whole menu area gets the black background (not just run cells)". So a naive "stop bridging" would regress both. The discriminator is presumably about what lies between the two runs — a rule/border being continued, versus two independent labels with the window's own ground between them. Establish it from evidence; do not pick by intuition.
