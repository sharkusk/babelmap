FOUND 2026-08-13 by `crates/app/examples/border_preview.rs`, the tool built to render every border set without playing to its room. **This is a weakness in SQ-0842's sweep, not in the renderer** — nothing the player sees is wrong.

## The defect

`border_corpus`'s `FLANK_WIDTHS = [46, 86]` are **unit columns measured on 640-pixel screens** (Shogun's and Zork Zero's flank widths). They are applied unscaled to every archive.

The Macintosh monochrome picture space is **480 wide**, and — critically — its art is REDRAWN for that space, not scaled from the 320-wide colour art (SQ-0838). So 86 columns is **18% of a mono screen** where the same constant is **13.4% of a colour one**. The crop lands deeper inside the border, hits solid opaque art on every row, `painted_widths` returns a 1.00 ratio, and `recognize` falls through to the slab arm.

Confirmed in both directions on the contact sheets:

* mono ids 34-40 and 163 show **no checkerboard at all** — a fully opaque crop — and classify SLAB, while their colour counterparts show frame-plus-gap and classify ZZPILLARS;
* mono id 6 (the vines) **flips within one archive**: SLAB at 46 columns, ZZPILLARS at 86.

## Why it matters

**42 of the monochrome archive's 72 "slabs" are a probing artefact.** So the sweep's pinned tally for that archive — `30 pillars / 42 slabs / 0 poles / 0 unrecognised` — is not measuring what it appears to: its coverage of `extend_pillars` on Macintosh art is materially weaker than the number implies, and a future regression in the pillar arm could hide behind flanks that were never really probed as pillars.

The tally pin still has value (it caught the SQ-0841 defect moving 28 MCGA and 28 Amiga flanks). It just must not be read as "42 of these are slabs".

## The fix, and one caution

Scale the probe width by the archive's own picture space: 86 x 480/640 ≈ **64** columns would probe a flank the Macintosh could actually have. Same for 46.

**Caution:** changing the widths will move the pinned tallies for every archive whose space is not 640 wide, and those pins are load-bearing. Re-pin deliberately, and state in the commit which numbers moved and why — a silent re-pin would destroy the regression signal that makes the sweep worth having.

## Two further observations from the same tool, neither a defect

1. **Colour plate 25's right flank routes to `ArthurPoles`** at both widths (art rows 2..398 — a two-pixel top inset, so `top == 0` fails and it falls past the slab arm). Already pinned as the corpus's only 2 poles. It composes cleanly; the sheet is simply the first place anyone can SEE what that arm produces on Zork Zero art.
2. **The extension inherits transparency from the art, correctly.** Colour ids 41, 49, 73, 99 show checkerboard inside the composed region; full-resolution inspection of plate 49 confirms the pillar tiles unbroken to row 900 and the transparent columns beside it are room interior the plate never painted, carried down by whole-row stamping as intended. Not a hole.

No composition defect was found in either archive: all 144 flanks filled their band to the last row, and the vines tile through 900 rows with no visible seam.
