Belongs in the `/info` diagnostic panel (standing policy: interesting diagnostic state goes into /info's spec as it is found), not a command of its own.

## What to report

The picker already queries the real cell size, so the panel can state:
- the cell in pixels (e.g. `6x12`),
- its ratio, and the signed distance from 2.000,
- and, because this is what a user can act on, the NEAREST font size whose rounded cell lands on exactly 2.000.

## Why the real cell and not the font's metrics

A terminal cell is `round(adv_em * px)` by `round(line_em * px)`, and those two round at different rates — the height crosses an integer boundary about twice as often, which is why scaling down often moves the height alone. The result is that the ratio a user actually gets swings far more than the font's own metrics suggest.

Measured on this machine (adv/em and ascent-descent+lineGap from hhea):

  FiraCode Nerd Font  em ratio 2.002, yet actual cell ratio ranges 1.750 (4x7 @ 6px) to 2.250 (4x9 @ 7px).
  Hits exactly 2.000 at 10 of 19 sizes in 6..24 px/em: cells 5x10, 6x12, 7x14, 8x16, 9x18, 10x20, 11x22, 13x26, 14x28, 15x30 — the historical terminal cell sizes.

  JetBrainsMono Nerd Font  em ratio 2.200. Hits 2.000 exactly ONCE in the same range (4x8 @ 6px), and is 10-29% off everywhere usable.

So the actionable guidance is not "change font" but "sit on a sweet spot", and only the real cell can tell you where those are. At 9 px/em FiraCode gives 6x11, so half-block samples are 6 wide by 5.5 tall and the artwork is sampled 9% coarser vertically; at 10 px/em it gives 6x12, samples exactly 6x6.

## Related

Ghostty's `adjust-cell-height` / `adjust-cell-width` can force the ratio for any font, so the panel naming the deviation makes that setting directly actionable. Also relevant to SQ-0963, which chose JetBrainsMono for gallery shots — a defensible choice for glyph coverage, but the worst of the measured set for half-block sample squareness.

## CORRECTION — one item in the note above is not implementable

The earlier note said the panel could name "the NEAREST font size whose rounded cell lands on exactly 2.000". **It cannot.** That needs adv/em and line/em, and the terminal never reports them.

Verified against ratatui-image's picker (the patched fork at 2365c61, src/picker.rs). Everything lanthorn can learn about the cell comes from three places, and all three yield the same single fact — cell size in PIXELS:

  1. `CSI 16 t` in the capability query (alongside `_Gi=` for kitty, `[c` for sixel, `[1337n` for iTerm2, `[5n` to guarantee a response) -> Response::CellSize.
  2. Fallback `font_size_fallback()`: rustix `tcgetwinsize`, then `FontSize::new(ws_xpixel / ws_col, ws_ypixel / ws_row)`.
  3. Neither answers -> hardcoded `FontSize::new(10, 20)`.

No font family, no PostScript name, no em metrics, no point size. The font measurements in the SQ-0963 note came from reading files on disk, which lanthorn cannot associate with the terminal's chosen face. Rounding has also already destroyed the information: a 6x12 cell is consistent with many fonts at many sizes.

## Revised scope — what IS implementable

- The cell in pixels, its ratio, and the signed deviation from 2.000 (square half-block samples).
- Which direction to nudge, since Ghostty's `adjust-cell-height`/`adjust-cell-width` act on exactly this.
- **Whether the value was MEASURED or ASSUMED.** This is the most valuable item and was not in the original scope. If both queries fail, every downstream geometry decision runs on a nominal 10x20 that may be nothing like the real cell — which is exactly what bit SQ-0973, where the nominal made the device box 4580x2880. A user seeing "cell 10x20 (assumed)" learns something actionable; seeing "10x20" alone learns something false.
- Worth surfacing per-platform: `font_size_fallback` is `None` on Windows, so a Windows terminal that does not answer `CSI 16 t` has no ioctl backstop and lands on the default.
