THE PRINCIPLE, from the user 2026-08-10, verbatim:

> "we have 3 main windows. the artwork on left, story window on the right, and the menu system on the bottom. the menu system should be treated as fixed 'y' size (pixels->terminal rows), but dynamic 'x'. The art and story are dynamic based on the width and the remaining height of the screen available above the menu. This is flipped compared to other v6, where our fixed size window is on the top."

So the rule for a v6 layout is: **one window is fixed-height and the rest take what remains.** In most v6 titles that fixed window is the status band at the TOP. In Journey it is the MENU at the BOTTOM. Same rule, inverted position — which is why Journey has been the title breaking every layout assumption in this run.

CONCRETELY:
- The menu's height in terminal rows is derived from its NATIVE PIXEL height (pixels → rows) and **does not vary** with what is left over.
- The menu's width is dynamic — it spans the pane.
- The art and story split the width between them, and take `pane_height − menu_rows` for their height.

WHAT THE CODE DOES TODAY — the inverse, measured from the user's own dumps:

    pane   scale   viewport rows        menu rows
    115    1.43    50  (rows 3..52)     9   (rows 53..61)
    157    1.96    47  (rows 3..49)     11  (rows 50..60)

Same pane height (61), different split. The **story viewport** is the scale-derived quantity — it follows the game's story window through the letterbox — and **the menu band is simply the remainder**. That is exactly backwards from the principle above, and it explains why the menu band's height wanders (9 vs 11) while its content stays a constant 7 game rows.

CONSEQUENCES TO WORK THROUGH WHEN THIS IS TAKEN UP:
1. This likely subsumes or reshapes **SQ-0754**. That quest's decision ("top anchor, grow the box with blank lines at the end") was made against a band whose height is leftover space. If the menu becomes fixed at its true native height, the leftover may shrink to nothing or to a constant, and the box-growing may become trivial or unnecessary. Re-measure before implementing SQ-0754 — do not build the box-growing on top of a height that is about to change.
2. It may also explain **SQ-0747 item C** (row 2's blank sliver) and **SQ-0758**-adjacent edge effects, since both are boundary artefacts of a split computed in the wrong direction.
3. The change is in the v6 layout planner around `BottomPlan::Menu`. Anything gated on `BottomPlan::Frame` (Zork Zero, Shogun) or `extend` (Arthur, advent, mysterious01) must be untouched by construction — prove it, as previous passes have.
4. Verify against the OTHER v6 titles that the top-fixed case is genuinely the same rule and not a separate code path. If it is separate, unifying them is the real fix and Journey is just the first title to expose the divergence.
