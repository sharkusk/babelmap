--- note 2026-08-12T18:08:41Z ---

`v6_border::pillar_shaft` finds a repeat unit as the longest run of rows holding one opaque column span. That is exactly right for a classical pillar (Zork Zero's castle: 70-73% of the flank on every rendition) and finds nothing usable in a surface with no plain span — the underground's alternating stone blocks and the jungle's foliage, which since SQ-0792 correctly fall back to the castle constants.

But those surfaces ARE periodic; they just are not CONSTANT. Bocfel's own numbers say so: `extend_underground_pillars(73, 54, 200, 74, 38, 37)` has a 38-raw-row stone course inside a 74-row pillar, and `extend_jungle_pillars(67, 210, 143, 59)` a 143-row strip with a 59-row overlap. An autocorrelation down the flank — smallest lag p minimising mean |row(y) - row(y+p)| over the art, under the same 16-row low-pass SQ-0808 uses to see through CGA's dither — would recover a repeat unit from ANY of the three without knowing which is on screen, and subsumes the constant-span case (a constant span autocorrelates at every lag, so the shaft still wins). This is the general tile-vs-stretch discriminator SQ-0698 deliberately dropped, arriving from the other end.

What it would NOT solve: the underground's left/right stone SWAP (Bocfel draws the right pillar's stones at x=0 and the left pillar's at x=width-37 on alternate tiles, so the two columns trade courses). That is a statement about the PAIR which only the scene identity justifies, and picture numbers do not survive the engine boundary — `WinNode::Graphics` in crates/app/src/engine.rs carries a flattened `Arc<RgbaImage>` and nothing else.

Worth doing only with the full corpus in front of it: 3 scenes x 4 native archives x 2 flanks for Zork Zero, plus Arthur's poles and Shogun's slab. `crates/app/tests/suites/v6_side_border_tiling.rs` case 9 already composes the scene borders from the archives' own pictures, so the harness exists. The bar is that it must reproduce the castle's current derivation — Bocfel's four constants (86 / 26 / 400 / 284 in unit space) to the row on the MCGA art — before it is allowed to replace it.
