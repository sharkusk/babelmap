--- note 2026-08-12T18:08:41Z ---

`v6_border::pillar_shaft` finds a repeat unit as the longest run of rows holding one opaque column span. That is exactly right for a classical pillar (Zork Zero's castle: 70-73% of the flank on every rendition) and finds nothing usable in a surface with no plain span — the underground's alternating stone blocks and the jungle's foliage, which since SQ-0792 correctly fall back to the castle constants.

But those surfaces ARE periodic; they just are not CONSTANT. Bocfel's own numbers say so: `extend_underground_pillars(73, 54, 200, 74, 38, 37)` has a 38-raw-row stone course inside a 74-row pillar, and `extend_jungle_pillars(67, 210, 143, 59)` a 143-row strip with a 59-row overlap. An autocorrelation down the flank — smallest lag p minimising mean |row(y) - row(y+p)| over the art, under the same 16-row low-pass SQ-0808 uses to see through CGA's dither — would recover a repeat unit from ANY of the three without knowing which is on screen, and subsumes the constant-span case (a constant span autocorrelates at every lag, so the shaft still wins). This is the general tile-vs-stretch discriminator SQ-0698 deliberately dropped, arriving from the other end.

What it would NOT solve: the underground's left/right stone SWAP (Bocfel draws the right pillar's stones at x=0 and the left pillar's at x=width-37 on alternate tiles, so the two columns trade courses). That is a statement about the PAIR which only the scene identity justifies, and picture numbers do not survive the engine boundary — `WinNode::Graphics` in crates/app/src/engine.rs carries a flattened `Arc<RgbaImage>` and nothing else.

Worth doing only with the full corpus in front of it: 3 scenes x 4 native archives x 2 flanks for Zork Zero, plus Arthur's poles and Shogun's slab. `crates/app/tests/suites/v6_side_border_tiling.rs` case 9 already composes the scene borders from the archives' own pictures, so the harness exists. The bar is that it must reproduce the castle's current derivation — Bocfel's four constants (86 / 26 / 400 / 284 in unit space) to the row on the MCGA art — before it is allowed to replace it.

--- note 2026-08-12T21:58:02Z ---

MEASURED, NEGATIVE RESULT — autocorrelation cannot replace the dispatch. Commit 4ced301f (branch worktree-agent-af454c7c6a3677265); no behaviour change.

The bar this quest set for itself — reproduce the castle's 86/26/400/284 on the MCGA art before replacing it — is met on nothing. Per-pixel autocorrelation over the composed scene flanks, below the top strip, (best lag, d') where d' is YIN's cumulative-mean-normalised difference and 1.0 is a coin toss:

  rendition   castle              underground        jungle
  zork0.mg1   4 (0.730/0.739)     74 (0.909/0.925)   84 (0.469/0.502)
  zork0.eg1   4 (1.009/1.004)     74 (0.813/0.745)   84 (0.381/0.384)
  zork0.cg1   44 (1.056/1.056)    76/74 (0.956/0.972) 84 (0.881/0.855)

against shipped repeat units of 284, 284/282 and 272. Driven through the real `zork_zero` handler on the in-game castle, the per-pixel form returns 4 on zork0.mg1 / zork0.pic / Zork0.blb and None on zork0.eg1 / zork0.cg1; the low-passed-luma form this quest specified returns None on 9 of those 10 flanks.

WHY, and it is structural, not tuning: a pillar shaft has no period. zork0.mg1's is uniform — rows pixel-identical, mean mismatch 0.30 of 255 at every lag 4..20 alike — so the search is maximally confident and maximally uninformative and returns the smallest lag offered. zork0.cg1's is a graded lit column (97->82, SQ-0808) and a gradient is not periodic either, so its best lag scores WORSE than the average lag. The statistic measures self-similarity; a plain shaft is more self-similar than patterned masonry, so it is anti-correlated with what it was asked to detect.

NO THRESHOLD EXISTS. Most confident castle flank 0.730 beats least confident underground flank 0.972 — accepts and rejects interleave. SQ-0792's cut sat in a 36%..70% gap; this one has no gap at all. Corroboration from outside Zork Zero: Arthur's poles (unit 4 rows cut at 90% height) score a spurious 64-row period at d'=0.451, more confident than 10 of these 12 flanks; Shogun's slab a spurious 114-row one at 0.90. Both are flanks whose repeat unit is not a period in the art but a choice of how much art to reuse — which autocorrelation cannot recover, since a unit appearing once has no lag.

Two more findings: (1) autocorrelation yields only the UNIT, never the top_cut or foot that extend_pillars also needs and that pillar_shaft does supply; (2) where it does fire on a target scene the two flanks of one symmetric border disagree (zork0.cg1 underground 76 L vs 74 R) — the asymmetry SQ-0792 removed. Also noted: with SQ-0808's mirror on, every join is a duplicated row, so case 7's seam oracle passes for ANY unit — a wrong unit shows as brightness banding, not a hard step, and no existing test sees it.

Shipped: v6_side_border_tiling.rs case 10 pins the corpus numbers and asserts both facts, so a future statistic that DOES separate the corpus fails the test and reopens this. Docs updated in v6-graphics.md and the v6_border module header. Gate 4383/4383 in 80.2s, clippy clean.
