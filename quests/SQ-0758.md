MEASURED by SQ-0750's crop fix, reported as found-not-fixed. BOTH profiles, so not an Amiga defect.

Journey, pane 163x61: pane columns 1..2, rows 3..48 carry only `menu_flank_panel`'s fill (Rgb(34,34,34)). The frame's outer-left edge simply does not exist between the `┌` on row 1 and the `└` on row 55. Under `BottomPlan::Menu` the flank is a fill plus the picture's bounding box only (SQ-0547), and the outer column falls outside it. The INNER divider survives solely because `flank_divider_extension` redraws it.

This completes the honest account of the user's "unmatched lines on the outside edges": the three edges are drawn by three different things — one is font glyphs, one is a scaled bitmap, and one is **nothing at all**.

SYMMETRIC WITH SQ-0747 item (A): the same `menu_flank_panel` fill runs 2 columns PAST the inner rule while stopping short of the outer edge. One fill, wrong on both sides. Whoever takes either should look at both — they are likely one boundary calculation, not two.

CORPUS CARE: `divider_exts` and this fill are gated on `BottomPlan::Menu`, so Zork Zero's and Shogun's flanks (`BottomPlan::Frame`) are untouched by construction. Arthur, advent and mysterious01 use `extend`. That gating is what makes this safe to fix, and any fix must preserve it.
