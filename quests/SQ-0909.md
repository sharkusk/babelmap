## What landed, and the finding under it

The absence of scrollback was self-inflicted. A terminal pushes a line into history only when the FULL screen scrolls; a line scrolled out of a DECSTBM region is **discarded**. Pinning the status bar therefore threw away every line of narrative the player had read.

Nothing is stored on our side. The rule is the one `quiet_status_line`'s doc already argued: **one row is chrome, taller is content.** One-row status bars flow into the transcript and reach history; menus, forms, InvisiClues panels and BeyondZork's compass still pin.

`/pin` toggles it, because height cannot settle every case: an InvisiClues menu is transient and releases itself, while BeyondZork's stats-and-compass is permanent chrome, so pinning it would cost scrollback for a whole game. That is not a property of the window — it is what the player is doing right now.

**The premise is measured, not asserted.** `screen::scrollback_tests` feeds `enter_region`/`leave_region`'s own output to `qwertty-term-vt` (Ghostty's core, the second opinion `app` already uses for image placement) and counts history: **0** rows survive a pinned run, a full screen's worth survives an unpinned one, and history resumes the moment the region is dropped — which is what makes `/pin` usable mid-session rather than a launch flag. Testing our renderer against our own decoder would only have proved it agrees with itself. Dev-dependency only; `cargo tree -p zvm-cli -e normal | grep -c qwertty` prints 0.

## What gvm-cli needs, and why it is not the same job

`glk_term.rs` confines a buffer window below grid windows with the same DECSTBM, but Glk layouts are arbitrary — grid above buffer, side-by-side, gutters, sub-column bands — and a grid window is usually genuine content rather than chrome. There is no honest one-row heuristic, so it wants the `/pin` toggle without the automatic rule, defaulting to pinned as it does today. The measurement above already covers the mechanism; what is left is the plumbing and deciding what `/pin` means when several windows are pinned at once.
