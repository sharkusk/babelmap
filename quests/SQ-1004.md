## The report

Screenshot: Arthur's InvisiClues hint screen off `Arthur - The Quest for Excalibur.adf` (the AMIGA floppy — name the release when reproducing; `real_media_releases.rs` pins it). Every line of the hint menu shows the game's blue ground **only in the cells a character was drawn in**, and the rest of each row is the host theme's dark:

- `N for next item.` / `P for previous item.` — blue behind the words, dark to the right of the full stop.
- `(Or use mouse.)`, `Return for hints.`, `Q to resume story.` — each its own blue island.
- `THE CHURCHYARD` reversed to a full-width white band, but `KING LOT`, `POINTS`, `NOTES` are short blue islands with dark to their right.

The user has a save state for it.

## Why this is NOT SQ-0954, though it looks like it

SQ-0954 is lanthorn's OWN annotation lines (`TranscriptKind::Meta`/`Warning`/`Input`) taking `period::painted`'s machine page instead of the story window's. This is the GAME's own text: the runs carry Arthur's blue correctly — that is why the glyph cells are right — and the defect is that nothing floods the row's remaining cells with the same ground. So the suspect is whatever paints the hint/menu band's rect, not the run styles.

## Where to look

`render/screen.rs` has a menu/hint path with its own band painting (`menu_band_rows`, `draw_chrome_text_strip`, `menu_flank_panel`); SQ-0946 just landed a fix in that area — two of those fills were bounded by the BAND rather than by the game's screen, and `screen_cols` was added in `v6_layout` to clip them. Check whether the hint screen goes through the takeover/menu arm at all: `picture_takeover_reason` and the "painted MENU screen" comment at `render/screen.rs:695` describe a v6 menu that prints chrome text INSIDE the story window's box, which hybrid must not draw as a pixel ring. Existing suites to read first and possibly extend: `v6_zork0_hints`, `v6_hint_menu_mouse`, `v6_arthur_*`.

Also worth checking against the real machine: `machine-screenshots/amiga-beyondzork.png` and the Arthur captures — a real Amiga fills the whole hint screen with the page, which is the claim to falsify.
