--- note 2026-08-09T17:22:36Z ---

Very likely resolved as a side effect of SQ-0715 (merged 2026-08-09, `4863a519`) — needs confirmation, not new work.

SQ-0715 replaced the unconditional `V6_ART_SCALE` doubling with a rule taken from Blorb §11: a resource file with no `Reso` chunk declares no scalable images, and non-scalable images display at actual size, one image pixel per screen pixel. `session.rs:800` now sets `art_scale = V6_ART_SCALE` only when the story is v6 AND `v6_screen_px` (the Reso standard window) is present, else 1.

Measured the corpus: Zork0.blb and Arthur.blb declare `Reso` std=320x200; **Mysterious01.blb and scopa.blb declare no Reso at all**. So mysterious01's 512x192 art now scales by 1 and lands at 512x192 on a 640x400 screen instead of the clipped 1024x384 this quest reported. This quest's own note wondered what discriminator to use given "no Reso chunk to key off" — the absence WAS the discriminator, and the spec says so.

Next step is a visual check of mysterious01's title card, not an implementation. If it renders unclipped, close this as done against SQ-0715's commit. Do NOT schedule the corpus dimension survey the earlier note proposed — the spec rule made it unnecessary.
