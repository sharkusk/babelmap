--- note 2026-08-12T06:34:31Z ---

MEASURED 2026-08-11 while implementing SQ-0790, on `stories/zork0-r393-s890714.z6` with `pictures = "zork0.eg1"`.

Now that the EGA plate lands at its true aspect, the geometry is right and the COLOURS are visibly wrong. Zork Zero's boot banner and pillars come out pink/magenta/olive; the same frame from `zork0.mg1` is the correct brown/bronze. Compare (both 640x400, same boot, same window rects):

    scratchpad/zork0-ega.png    pink banner, pink/green speckled pillars
    scratchpad/zork0-mcga.png   brown banner, bronze pillars
    scratchpad/zork0-cga.png    same problem

The cause is already written down in `crates/blorb/src/infocom_pics.rs`, so this is a KNOWN gap rather than a discovery — it just had no visible symptom until the art was the right shape:

- `DEFAULT_PALETTE` doc: "It is *not* claimed to be the IBM EGA or CGA hardware table, and a PC EGA or CGA archive — which stores no palettes of its own — should be drawn through [one]."
- `Picture::rgba` falls back to `DEFAULT_PALETTE` when the record has no palette of its own.
- A PC EGA/CGA archive has 12-byte directory records with NO `palOff` at all (only MCGA and the Amiga/Mac use the 14-byte form), so EVERY EGA/CGA picture takes that fallback.

So the fix is a caller supplying the hardware table, which is the missing half of "the art you name determines the machine you are emulating": naming `.EG1` should bring the EGA palette with it, `.CG1` the CGA one. Both are small fixed tables and both must come from an authoritative source rather than memory — bocfel has them (`terps/bocfel/z6/`, and note its `monochrome_black`/`monochrome_white` handling for the CGA `EF_MONO` records, which is a second question this quest should answer), and Frotz's `src/curses/ux_pic.c` is a second witness.

Two things to be careful of, both already load-bearing elsewhere:
1. CGA is not simply "EGA with fewer colours" — `infocom_pics.rs` documents that Zork Zero's whole CGA archive is byte-per-index while Arthur/Journey/Shogun keep large CGA artwork in a packed form, with `EF_MONO` records going straight into the CGA framebuffer where bit 7 is the intensity bit.
2. The adaptive/Current-Palette machinery (Blorb 11.3, SQ-0743) must not start splicing a PLTE into pictures whose colours the hardware fixes.

Not a regression from SQ-0790 — it was equally wrong before, just harder to notice because the plate was the wrong shape anyway.
