--- note 2026-08-10T04:48:07Z ---

SHARPER SYMPTOM, user 2026-08-09 (now that SQ-0744 has landed and Shogun's floppy actually supplies art): "amiga shogun doesn't show the centered text as the menu is rendered at the very top of the page, pushing the centered text off the top of the screen. you can briefly see it if you scroll up (but it snaps back down)."

Two facts, and the second is the important one.

1. The centred text is not lost — it is ABOVE the viewport. Scrolling up reveals it.
2. **It snaps back down.** That is the transcript bottom-sticking: the view is pinned to the end of the transcript, so content above the visible rows is unreachable and any manual scroll is immediately overridden.

So this is a PROSE ANCHORING defect, not a menu placement defect. The original framing on this quest — "the menu is drawn on the top line instead of beside the text" — reads the symptom backwards. The menu is where it is; the text has been pushed out from under it and the view will not stay where it would show it.

SAME FAMILY AS TWO OTHER LIVE ITEMS, worth checking together before anyone treats this as a Shogun quirk:
- SQ-0748 (Beyond Zork, FIXED) was exactly this mechanism inverted — `anchor_row_at` returned `None` for an anchor AT the end of the transcript, and `window_wrapped_rows` fell through to bottom-sticking the scrollback the game had just erased. Bottom-sticking-when-it-should-not is the shared failure.
- SQ-0755 (Journey, OPEN) has prose landing at the BOTTOM of the pane over the menu blocks. Journey's prose goes too far down; Shogun's goes off the top because the view sticks to the bottom. Both are "where does the prose block anchor" defects and may share a cause.
