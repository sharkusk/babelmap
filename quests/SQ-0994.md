## The organising principle: separate what was MEASURED from what was ASSUMED

That distinction is the reason this is worth building, and it should shape the layout rather than being a footnote. Several values lanthorn runs on are guesses that look exactly like measurements, and a user cannot currently tell which is which:

- The cell size falls back to a hardcoded 10x20 when `CSI 16 t` goes unanswered — and on Windows there is no ioctl backstop at all. `cell 10x20` and `cell 10x20 (assumed)` mean completely different things, and every graphics geometry decision downstream is computed from it. This is exactly what bit SQ-0973, where the nominal 10x20 made a device box 4580x2880.
- A capability list is empty both when the terminal said "no" and when nobody asked (non-tty, `halfblocks()`, tmux without passthrough).
- The interpreter profile may come from the medium, from a user answer, or from a fallback — `ProfileSource::{Medium, Asked, Fallback}` already distinguishes these and `licenses_machine_colours` already turns on it.

## What to report (accumulated across several quests)

**Terminal**
- Protocol in use, and whether auto-detected or forced by `--image-protocol`.
- Cell size in pixels, **and whether measured (`CSI 16 t`), derived (TIOCGWINSZ), or assumed (10x20 default)**.
- Cell aspect and its signed distance from 2.000 — the ratio that makes a half-block sample square. Note a cell is `round(adv*px)` by `round(line*px)` and the two round at different rates, so real cells swing 1.75..2.25 even for a font whose design ratio is exactly 2.002. Ghostty's `adjust-cell-height`/`adjust-cell-width` act directly on this, so naming the deviation is actionable. (This was SQ-0986, which should be folded in here and closed as subsumed — its own note carries the measurements.)
- Detected capabilities: `Kitty`, `Sixel`, `RectangularOps`, `TextSizingProtocol`, `CellSize`, `Background`, and `KittyCompression`.
- **Whether kitty uploads are actually being compressed** — the question that prompted this. `KittyCompression` present means `o=z` is on the wire; absent means raw. Failure is silent either way, which is why it needs surfacing.

**Story**
- Engine, Z-machine Version or Glulx, release and serial, and the medium it was mounted from.
- Interpreter profile, its `ProfileSource`, the palette it resolves colour numbers through, and `honor_game_colours`.

**v6 render**
- Hybrid vs raster and which `picture_takeover_reason` fired.
- Native screen size, `art_scale`, current magnification, and whether the pixel lock is on.

**Traffic**, if cheap to carry: bytes emitted this session, and the compressed/raw ratio when compression is on. SQ-0977 measured that under half-blocks ~80% of the stream is SGR, and under kitty the placeholder cells now cost more than the compressed images do — both are the kind of thing a user reporting "it feels slow" could paste back.

## Constraints

- One slash command in the single `slash::COMMANDS` registry (`crates/app/src/slash.rs`) — verb-noun, no Command enum.
- Every new UI element must be styleable via a `style.toml` selector; never hard-code a colour.
- Do not add per-frame cost to gather any of it. If a statistic is not already tracked, say so rather than adding a counter to the render path.

--- note 2026-08-22T03:29:31Z ---

SHIPPED as `/dump-terminal` (f44d63f6), renamed and rescoped mid-lane on the user's call.

RENAME: every command in `slash::COMMANDS` is verb-noun, and `dump-cells` / `dump-windows` / `dump-notifications` are already exactly this kind of diagnostic — `/info` was neither, and this completes the family rather than starting a new one.

SCOPE, narrowed: the STORY bibliographic section (release, serial, medium, `ProfileSource`, `honor_game_colours`) is NOT in the shipped command. That is story identity, not terminal, and three things wearing one name. It remains unbuilt and unclaimed — if it is still wanted, it wants its own quest and probably its own command. Render state is kept only insofar as it explains the traffic (hybrid vs raster + takeover arm, native screen + art_scale, magnification + pixel lock, story-pane cells).

STATS were promoted to a first-class part of the command: bytes this session, bytes for the last drawn frame, mean per frame, frame flushes, graphics ops (upload/reuse/place/drop) and the placeholder cells under this frame's placements, chrome-band uploads since launch. Bytes come from a `CountingWriter` wrapping the ratatui backend — one `fetch_add` per `write`, one per `flush`, never inspecting a byte. Escapes written straight to stdout (alternate screen, mouse mode) bypass it, and the report says so.

REPORTED AS UNAVAILABLE, deliberately:
- compressed-vs-raw upload sizes. Neither encoder reports the two lengths; measuring it means instrumenting `kitty_transmit_virtual` AND the vendored ratatui-image fork's `transmit_virtual`. The o=z lines say whether compression is ON, not what it bought.
- an APC `\x1b_G` count taken off the wire. The `GraphicsOp` log answers the same question for free; scanning the stream would be O(bytes) on the frame path.

FILE MIRROR: `~/.lanthorn/dump-terminal.log`, appended and timestamped, path named in the transcript, `append_terminal_dump` beside `append_window_dump`. This is the copy that goes into a bug report — the on-screen copy of a v6 pane cannot be selected without dragging kitty placeholder glyphs along (SQ-0756).

TWO SELECTORS: `terminal_dump_heading` (parent `heading`, bold) and `terminal_dump_assumed` (parent `alert`), the latter on every line carrying a guessed or unreachable value. `style.example.toml` regenerated.

ALSO FIXED, found the hard way: `dump-terminal` had to join `DEFAULT_DIRECT_COMMANDS` in `keymap.rs`. `is_direct_name` gates every Ctrl binding, and Ctrl is the only class of key the char-mode gate lets past a story waiting on a keypress — so a bound key for it did nothing at all under Journey's menu. `dump-windows` and `dump-cells` are already there for the same reason; here it is sharper still, because reaching this command through the palette is itself traffic and bytes-per-frame taken that way describes the palette's frame.

SQ-0986 is genuinely subsumed: the cell aspect and its signed distance from 2.000 ship here, and its correction (naming "the nearest font size that lands on 2.000" is not implementable) was honoured — the report never claims one.

FOUND, OUT OF LANE, NOT FIXED: `kitty_transmit_virtual` (`render/graphics.rs:2550`) states `o=z` UNCONDITIONALLY, so a terminal that advertises kitty and refuses `o=z` loses every graphics-window image silently — the exact failure SQ-0991 chose to avoid on the ratatui-image path by asking the capability. That file belongs to the SQ-0995 lane, so it was left alone; the command reports both encoders separately and says which of them asks.

CONFIRMED by running it. `/dump-terminal` exists, works against a real pty capture, and mirrors to `~/.lanthorn/dump-terminal.log` the way `/dump-windows` does.

Real output (Journey r30 `.adf`, 117x64, kitty negotiated):

    terminal
      graphics protocol: kitty (auto-detected)
      cell size: 8x16 px — MEASURED (the terminal answered CSI 16 t)
      cell aspect: 2.000 (height/width), +0.000 from the 2.000 that makes a half-block sample square
        CSI 16 t answered: 8x16 px
        TIOCGWINSZ says now: 8x16 px
      capabilities (3), as answered: Kitty, KittyCompression, CellSize(8x16 px, from CSI 16 t)
      kitty transmission compression (o=z):
        ratatui-image uploads: COMPRESSED — the terminal answered the o=z probe
        graphics-window uploads: COMPRESSED — unconditionally; that path states o=z whatever the probe said
    render
      story pane: 218x63 cells = 13,734 cell(s)
      v6 mode: hybrid / picture takeover: art_paints_anything — the last frame fell through to raster
      native screen: 640x400 game pixels, art_scale 2x2 / magnification 1.000x, pixel lock off
    traffic
      bytes written to the terminal: 201,393 in 9 frame flush(es) since launch
      placeholder cells under those placements: 16,000

**The measured-vs-assumed distinction works and is the point of the command**: the report names the SOURCE of the cell (MEASURED / DERIVED / assumed) and shows both what `CSI 16 t` answered and what `TIOCGWINSZ` says now, so a stale launch-time answer is visible rather than silently authoritative.

**It is also already reporting a real defect in its own output** — the "graphics-window uploads: COMPRESSED — unconditionally" line is SQ-0997, found by this lane and filed. A diagnostic that announces a bug nobody had fixed yet is behaving correctly.

Honest about its limits, which is why it is trustworthy: compressed-vs-raw upload sizes are reported as unavailable WITH THE REASON (neither encoder tracks both lengths), and the placeholder-cell count states it came from the render's op log rather than from scanning the wire.

Left unbuilt and unclaimed: the story section originally specced here — release, serial, medium, `ProfileSource`, `honor_game_colours` — dropped when the command was scoped to the terminal and renamed from `/info`. It wants its own quest and probably its own command.
