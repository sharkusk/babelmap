**REOPENED 2026-08-16. The earlier fix and its test were both measured on a screen the app never gives this press.**

`ring_scout` and `v6_side_border_tiling.rs`'s `boot()` both skipped `picts.native_std_window()` in `startup.rs`'s screen-size chain, so Arthur r63 — a **560x384** press — was booted at 640x400 and the GAME laid its own windows out to fit. The "single illustration standing clear of both edges" frame that the withdrawn case asserted about does not exist: on 560x384, six turns in is the ordinary gameplay frame with poles at both edges. Fixed in `7fad624a`; the case's own non-vacuity guard is what caught it ("the art runs 0..=559 of 560 native columns").

The edge-contact and mid-bound guards added to `flank_art_columns` in `fe43d936` are left in place — they are defensible and pinned synthetically by `render::screen::tests::a_flanks_columns_come_from_the_narrowest_row_of_its_art` — but they were never this defect.

## The real defect, measured on the user's own frame

From their `/dump-windows` at a **129x60 pane** (`stories/Arthur.po`, release 63 serial 890622, `AppleIIgs off ProDos`), and reproduced by `cargo run -q -p app --example ring_scout -- --story stories/Arthur.po --keys n --taps 6 --size 129x60 --bands`:

```
strip:art 4x20 at (1,1)          band 4x20@(1,1)  · native 18x195@(0,0)
strip:art 4x39 at (1,22)         band 4x39@(1,22) [Art, tiled] · source 17x381 native px · into 32x702px
```

The flank band occupies pane rows 22..60, i.e. device y 378..1080, i.e. **native y 205..586**. Its source is `17x381` — native rows **0..381**, taken from the column's own top. So the band paints the top of the column, banner cap and all, starting at device row 378, and the cap the band above already drew at rows 1..20 is drawn a **second time** partway down the flank. That is the "banner copies in flank" as reported.

At a taller pane it is unmistakable: `--taps 8 --size 129x60` composes `source 17x586 native px` on a **384-row screen** — a strip half again as long as the whole screen, so the column is tiled through its own cap more than once.

The magnifications agree throughout (702/381 = 1.843 against the frame's s = 1.8429), so SQ-0898's invariant is not violated. This is source SELECTION, not scaling.

## Why Arthur's other presses look right

`Arthur - The Quest for Excalibur.adf` (r54, 640x400) has the same band shape — `5x23@(0,14)` from `source 33x338` — but its poles run native 11..379 of 400, so the column is nearly uniform full height and a repeat reads as more pole rather than as a duplicated cap. `v6_border::recognize` classifies that press as `ArthurPoles` and `flank_source` cuts a proper repeat unit for it. The ProDOS press has a distinctive cap at the column's top and a 384-row screen, and the recogniser's bottom test is the thing to check first: the docs table records Arthur's DOS press clearing it "by exactly one row — 384 + 16 is 400, which is not GREATER than 400", and on a 384-row screen 381 + 17 = 398 > 384 goes the other way.

Start at `crates/app/src/render/v6_border.rs`: `recognize()` and `flank_source()`, and the caller `flank_tiled_source` in `screen.rs`. Verify on the 560x384 screen, at the user's 129x60 pane and at least one taller one, and pin the fix with a case in `v6_side_border_tiling.rs` in the place the withdrawn one left.
