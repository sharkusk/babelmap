MEASURED 2026-08-15 on `155b9b91`, via a temporary probe over `shogun-r322-s890706.z6`'s credits/menu screen (probe reverted, tree clean).

**THE GAME DOES NOT BAKE IN A FONT SIZE — IT ASKS US.** V6 games measure a string by printing it to output stream 3 and reading the width back from header `$30` (ZMSD §15; our implementation is `crates/zvm/src/screen.rs:1374`, cited at `crates/zvm/src/cpu/exec.rs:2499`). We answer `chars × V6_FONT_WIDTH` — a MONOSPACE assumption we supply, pinned by tests asserting `8 * V6_FONT_WIDTH` and `6 * V6_FONT_WIDTH` at `screen.rs:2816`/`:2834`. `V6_FONT_WIDTH = 8`, `V6_FONT_HEIGHT = 16` (`screen.rs:1894`), sourced from Frotz's DOS mode table `{0x12, 640, 400, 8, 16}` for the authentic 80x25 (SQ-0479).

This is what the user's observation exposed: **Spatterlight centres this same text with a PROPORTIONAL font.** That is only possible because the game asks rather than assumes — Spatterlight reports its own glyph widths and the game centres correctly for them. Nothing is hardcoded anywhere in the story.

THE PROBE DATA (coordinates are 1-BASED, so subtract one before taking the modulus):

| run | chars | `(x-1) % 8` |
|---|---|---|
| `SHOGUN` | 6 | **0** |
| `A Story of Japan` | 16 | **0** |
| `All rights reserved.` | 20 | **0** |
| `SHOGUN is a trademark of James Clavell` | 38 | **0** |
| `Copyright (c) 1988 by Infocom` | **29** | **4** |
| menu single-char runs (`S`,`T`,`A`,`R`,`T`,…) | — | **2** |

Every `y` satisfies `(y-1) % 16 == 0` — vertically perfect. Horizontal pitch is exactly 8. **Even-length centred strings land dead on a cell; the one ODD-length line lands on a half-cell**, because centring is `(80 − 29)/2 = 25.5` cells. That parity comes from the DIVISION, not from the metric, so it survives any font width we report — changing `V6_FONT_WIDTH` cannot fix it.

**WHY THE EARLIER RING ATTEMPT FAILED, correctly diagnosed at last.** SQ-0886's lane tried the ring and got `"SI(RT th e ga me"`, and both that lane and I read this as "pixel-positioned text cannot be a cell grid". WRONG. The menu is emitted as 15 single-character runs at 8px pitch sharing one origin offset of +2px — a QUARTER of a cell. Rounding each run INDEPENDENTLY makes neighbours collide into one cell or skip one, which is exactly the doubled-and-gapped signature. A uniform 2px shift is invisible.

**THE FIX: quantize runs that share a baseline as a GROUP with one shared origin, not per run.** `START the game` then stays intact, merely shifted by a quarter cell. That is what SQ-0750 actually asks for — text as text, art as art — and it makes hybrid viable on screens that currently fall to the composite.

WHAT MUST BE ESTABLISHED, not assumed:
  - How to group. Sharing a `y` baseline is the obvious key, but a screen may legitimately carry two separate blocks on one baseline (a left label and a right menu column at the same y — this very screen has `You may choose to:` beside the menu). Grouping those under one origin would drag one of them sideways. Find the rule that keeps them apart; a gap threshold in whole cells is the likely shape, but MEASURE the corpus before fixing a constant.
  - Whether the odd-length half-cell line can be absorbed the same way, or needs its own rounding decision.
  - Whether any v6 screen relies on sub-cell placement in a way group-rounding visibly breaks. The corpus sweep is the evidence.

DO NOT change `V6_FONT_WIDTH`/`V6_FONT_HEIGHT`. They are sourced, they give the authentic 80x25, and per the arithmetic above they cannot fix the parity anyway.

CLEANUP MANDATE FROM THE USER, and it is part of this quest, not a follow-up: once hybrid can render these screens, review the code and REMOVE what is no longer needed. Prime candidates, each to be judged by MEASUREMENT rather than by reasoning:
  - `menu_over_art` (SQ-0886, `d1ffc8a0`, in `crates/app/src/render/screen.rs`) — the predicate that excludes such a frame from the cell escape and the ring so it falls to the composite. If the ring can now draw the frame correctly, this may be dead.
  - The `frozen_head` suppression (SQ-0890, `155b9b91`, `interleave_story_elems` + `Machine::v6_prose_retired_whole` at `crates/zvm/src/cpu/exec.rs:318`) — it exists because the composite gave the screen a 548x64 story box. If the frame stops taking the composite, the duplication it suppresses may not arise.
  - Anything else the change strands.

THE PRECEDENT FOR HOW TO DO THAT REMOVAL WELL is SQ-0888's lane: it deleted `story_text_box` only after measuring that it answered `None` on every corpus frame at boot and on every turn. Measure first, delete second, and state the measurement. **Do NOT delete a mechanism merely because it looks superseded** — and do not delete its tests to make a removal pass; retarget them to the new behaviour or say plainly why they no longer describe anything.

ACCEPTANCE: the credits/menu screen renders in hybrid with the ornate side panels present, the credits and menu as CRISP GLYPHS (not rasterised), no duplicated text, no collision, and `You may choose to:` on the first screen. Compare against the composite renders captured on `155b9b91` — those are correct in content and are the bar to match or beat.

--- note 2026-08-15T19:05:18Z ---

THE LEGITIMATE RASTER CASE, from the user 2026-08-15 — this bounds the fix and every verdict in the audit.

**Text composited ON TOP OF GRAPHICS must be rastered.** Their example: Zork Zero's location/score text drawn onto the ribbon art. This is already the documented rationale for the whole `px_texts` channel, `crates/app/src/engine.rs:168`: *"pixel raster draws status text where the game placed it (Zork Zero puts its banner text at rows 6/14, ON the ribbon art) instead of snapping to the char grid."*

**SO THE CRITERION IS "IS THERE ART BENEATH THIS TEXT?", NOT "IS THIS TEXT PIXEL-POSITIONED?"** That is the sharp version of the test this quest falsified, and it cleanly separates the two cases:

| | beneath the text | correct treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Shogun credits/menu | the machine's plain ground | **glyphs** — the runs account for every pixel |
| Zork Zero banner | the ribbon artwork | **raster** — no glyph can reproduce what is under it |

A glyph in a terminal cell carries its own background, which would occlude artwork beneath it. That is the physical reason the exception exists, and it is not a limitation to engineer around.

This is also the precise sharpening of SQ-0750's standard — *"reserve raster for pixels the runs cannot account for"*. Text over art is exactly a case where the runs cannot account for the pixels. Text over a plain ground is exactly the opposite.

TWO CONSEQUENCES, both now required of the lane:
  - The quantization predicate must EXCLUDE runs with opaque art beneath them, and Zork Zero's banner must be verified by measurement to still rasterise as part of the corpus sweep. Turning it into glyphs on a flat background would be a regression even though the text would read as "crisper".
  - The inventory must state this distinction explicitly per entry: art beneath = **correctly rasterising**, plain ground = **candidate**. That distinction is the survey's whole value.

The pairing — Shogun's credits become glyphs, Zork Zero's banner stays raster — is the clearest available demonstration that the new predicate discriminates rather than converting everything, and should be in the test coverage.
