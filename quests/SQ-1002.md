## The rule that is being violated

A v6 game sizes its artwork in NATIVE PIXELS, laid out beside text it sizes in NATIVE CHARACTER CELLS. Zork Zero's drop cap is drawn to stand about three or four lines of prose tall. That proportion holds only when

    magnification  ==  device_cell / native_cell

In hybrid mode lanthorn scales the two independently: **art is magnified to fill the pane**, while **text is drawn at whatever the terminal's cell happens to be**. Nothing ties them. So the larger the pane, the further the art outgrows the prose — which is the reported symptom and it gets worse as the window grows.

Measured on `zork0-dos.png` (the gallery, but the app shows it too — user confirmed): footer reads `162x53 cells at 8x16px` and `640x400 native at 2.000x`. lanthorn assumes a native v6 cell of 8x16 (SQ-0917's subject), so at 2x magnification the text cell should be 16x32. It is 8x16 — **text is exactly half-scale relative to the art on both axes**, which is why the cap spans about eight lines instead of four.

The cap is not huge. Everything else is small.

## Consequences worth thinking through before fixing

- **Filling the pane and preserving proportion are mutually exclusive.** Magnification cannot be chosen for both. Either the frame letterboxes (art at the proportion the game drew, unused pane around it) or the art is deliberately out of proportion with the prose. There is no third option while text is terminal glyphs.
- **The correct magnification is therefore derived from the cell, not from the box**: `M = device_cell / native_cell`, which is 1 on an ordinary 8x16 terminal cell and 2 on a 16x32 one. That is close in spirit to the pixel lock (SQ-0945) but anchored to the cell rather than to the artwork's own ladder.
- **This interacts with SQ-0917.** The 8x16 native cell is an assumption; if a press really used something else, the multiplier moves with it and the current proportion is wrong by a different factor per machine.
- **Raster mode is not affected in the same way** — there the text is rasterised too, so both scale together. That is worth confirming, and if true it means the defect is specific to hybrid's mixed model.
- Check what half-blocks does: its "cell" is the sample grid, so the relationship may differ again.

## Do not fix by shrinking the art

The instinct will be to cap magnification. That is the same lever SQ-0964 deliberately removed for half-blocks and would undo real work. The question is not "how big should the art be" but "what magnification keeps art and text agreeing", and the answer is a ratio, not a ceiling.
