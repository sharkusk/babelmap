MEASURED 2026-08-12 (the measurement this quest asked for first), by a different route than the quest proposed.

Reaching the underground or the jungle in-game is a long play session, so instead the flanks were COMPOSED from each archive's own pictures exactly as DISPLAY_BORDER draws them: top strip (picture 7 underground, 6 jungle, 5 castle) at (0,0), then the left pillar 0x1f3/0x1f5/0x1f1 and the right 0x1f4/0x1f6/0x1f2 at y = strip height, into a 640x400 unit-space canvas. The flank width is the same for all three scenes because one TEXT_WINDOW_PIC_LOC picture fixes it, so the castle's in-game width (86 px; 88 on zork0.pic) is the right one to read them at. The castle composed this way reproduces the in-game shaft to the row (mg1 [82,374), cg1 [102,382)), which is what makes the other two trustworthy.

So SQ-0799's derivation, which is exactly right for the castle, DECLINES or returns a spurious 12-to-146-row run on the other two — the underground is alternating stone blocks and the jungle is foliage, and neither holds one opaque width for long. Where it declines the castle constants stand (the quest's own prediction, and they do degrade gracefully); where it returns a spurious run the two flanks get different recipes from each other, which is worse and is new information. Left and right disagreeing is visible in the table above on mg1 and cg1 underground.

WHAT CHANGED ANYWAY: SQ-0808 turned the pillar repeat into a MIRRORED one (`extend_pillars(..., flip=true)`), so every internal tile join is now an exact duplicated row for whichever border is on screen. That is precisely what Bocfel's per-scene overlaps exist to approximate — 37 raw rows underground, 59 in the jungle, the largest of the three — so the seam those numbers were chosen to hide is gone without knowing which scene we are in. The REPEAT UNIT is still the castle's.

STILL OPEN, and it is the same wall the quest named: `extend_underground_pillars(73, 54, 200, 74, 38, 37)` alternates the left and right stone blocks between tiles so the masonry stays consistent, and no pixel measurement recovers that — it needs to know the border is the underground one, which is a Z-machine global the renderer cannot see. Worth noting the jungle art is 210 raw rows, taller than the 200-row screen, so its base is clipped off the native canvas before we ever see it; the same is true of the CGA castle pillar (total_height 205 = banner 39 + pillar 166), whose 25-row foot survives only 9 rows on screen.

RELATED, FIXED HERE: `recognize` now also measures the flank's narrowest-to-widest painted row (SQ-0802). The underground and jungle flanks measure 0.77-0.81 and 0.37-0.80, comfortably inside the pillar side of the 9/10 cut, so promoting that test did NOT reclassify them — which was the hazard, since the cg1 underground pillar is 86 px wide against an 86 px flank and any test keyed on the pillar being narrow than the flank alone would have failed it.

VERDICT on "is the scene dispatch solvable without the border global": PARTLY, and the solvable half was where the regression was.

* CASTLE vs NOT-CASTLE — solvable, and now solved. Reproduced the prior lane's composition (strip 5/6/7 at 0,0; pillars 0x1f1..0x1f6 at y = strip height; flank width = the castle's in-game 86 px, 88 on zork0.pic) and confirmed its table to the row.       scene         zork0.mg1   zork0.eg1   zork0.cg1   zork0.pic
      castle        292 / 292   292 / 290   280 / 280   292 / 292
      underground    54 / 146    44 / 180    54 /  72    54 /  76

  Castle = 70-73% everywhere; the longest non-castle run that `pillar_shaft` would otherwise have ACCEPTED is 146 rows = 36% (the eg1 180 and 44 already fail the existing top==0 / bottom>=art_bottom guards). Cut at HALF the flank: in the gap, and it is the definition of a pillar rather than a fitted number. Castle keeps its SQ-0799 derivation on all five renditions; underground and jungle fall back to the castle constants UNIFORMLY.

* THE REAL DEFECT was asymmetry, not the wrong unit. Six of the eight non-castle flank PAIRS got different recipes from each other — cg1 underground cut left at 78 and right at 296; mg1 jungle derived a 14-row unit left and fell back to 284 right. A border is one pair of pictures at one y, so the two flanks must agree. They now do.

* THE JUNGLE ROUTINE IS LARGELY MOOT. extend_jungle_pillars(67, 210, 143, 59) is a plain overlap repeat with no foot, and the 59-row overlap exists to hide the seam SQ-0808's mirror removes outright. Its total_height of 210 raw rows is taller than the 200-row screen, so the rows it reads below 200 are clipped from our canvas before we receive it — Bocfel sees them only because its pixmap stays unclipped until flush_bitmap.

* GENUINELY BLOCKED: the underground's stone alternation. Read the reference rather than the summary — extend_underground_pillars does NOT flip its tile, it SWAPS the flanks: on alternate tiles it draws the right pillar's 37-px-wide stones at x=0 and the left pillar's at x=width-37, so the two columns trade courses. That is a statement about the PAIR, applied only to the underground, and applying it to the castle would flip a lit column's shading. Nothing in the pixels says "this is the underground". Confirmed the block at the boundary: `WinNode::Graphics` (crates/app/src/engine.rs) carries a flattened `Arc<RgbaImage>` and nothing else — picture numbers do not survive into the app at all, so this is not a plumbing oversight.

* SHAPE OF A FUTURE ANSWER, not taken here: the underground's stone course is periodic (38 raw rows), so autocorrelation down a flank would yield a scene-agnostic repeat unit and make the dispatch unnecessary. That would replace SQ-0799's measured castle derivation wholesale and wants its own quest.

Tests: `zork_zeros_other_two_scene_borders_declare_no_shaft_and_agree_across_flanks` (v6_side_border_tiling.rs, case 9) composes all three scenes on all four native archives and asserts the castle composed == the castle in-game before trusting the rest; plus a synthetic unit case in v6_border.rs.
