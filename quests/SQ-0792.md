  SQ-0698 landed side-border tiling with per-title handlers. Zork Zero's handler uses Bocfel's CASTLE constants only: extend_pillars(43, 13, 200, 142, 0, false, false) on the VGA/Amiga/Blorb art, doubled into unit space as top_cut=86, foot=26, unit=284, overlap=0, flip=false. Bocfel dispatches on the Z-machine's own border global and has two more routines: extend_underground_pillars(73, 54, 200, 74, 38, 37), which alternates left/right stone blocks so the masonry stays consistent, and extend_jungle_pillars(67, 210, 143, 59) — note the jungle art is 210 raw rows, TALLER than the 200-row screen, and its 59-row overlap is the largest of the three. We cannot read that global: the renderer sees a pixel canvas, not picture numbers. The castle constants degrade gracefully (row 86 is below the capital in all three; the bottom 26 rows are the base in all three) but a jungle screen loses its overlap and may seam. Measure first: capture Zork Zero in the underground and outside the castle at a tall pane and look at the join.
