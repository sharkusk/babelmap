Measured surface, 2026-08-14, from each binary's own --help / arg table:

babelmap   --config --data-dir --debug --image-protocol --interpreter-number --no-accel --no-images --no-sound --pictures --trace --user-dir
zvm-cli    --data-dir --interpreter --lower-only --no-aux --no-game-colours --no-more --no-page --no-sound --no-status --no-timed-input --plain --screen-reader --show-status --story --story-only --volume
gvm-cli    --data-dir --no-accel --no-game-colours --no-more --no-page --plain --screen-reader --show-status --story-only
scott-cli  --max-turns --no-more --no-page --plain --screen-reader --seed

THE DECIDED CHANGE: babelmap's --interpreter-number becomes --interpreter, matching zvm-cli. Pre-release, so rename outright — no deprecated alias, per the project's no-back-compat rule. The old spelling appears throughout doc comments in config.rs and in docs/, so it needs a sweep, not just an arg-table edit.

ALREADY CONSISTENT, leave alone: --data-dir, --no-sound, --no-accel, --no-more, --no-page, --plain, --screen-reader, --show-status, --story-only.

THE REAL GAP FOUND WHILE MEASURING: --no-game-colours exists on zvm-cli and gvm-cli but NOT on babelmap, even though babelmap owns the honor_game_colours config and a /set-game-colours command. Every render suite in this project pins both colour modes precisely because that switch matters; not being able to launch the TUI into the declining mode is an asymmetry worth closing. Treat as a question for the user rather than an automatic addition.

NOT divergences, do not "fix": --lower-only, --no-aux, --no-status, --no-timed-input, --volume, --story, --max-turns, --seed are engine- or CLI-specific; --config, --user-dir, --image-protocol, --no-images, --pictures, --debug, --trace are TUI-specific. The test is whether two binaries express the SAME concept under different names, not whether one has a flag the other lacks.
