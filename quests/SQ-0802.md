MEASURED 2026-08-12 while fixing SQ-0799, on `stories/shogun-r322-s890706.z6` at a gameplay frame (12 blank turns), left and right flanks both.

Shogun's border art is a DIFFERENT HEIGHT in every rendition, and two of them reach the native screen bottom:

    archive        pic 3 (P_BORDER) raw   unit rows   flank art_extent   recognize()
    Pic.data/.adf  (Amiga) 23x168          336         (0, 336)          ShogunSinglePiece  <- correct
    Shogun.blb     (Blorb)                 400         (0, 400)          ZorkZeroPillars    <- wrong
    shogun.mg1     23x200                  400         (0, 400)          ZorkZeroPillars    <- wrong
    shogun.eg1     46x200                  400         (0, 400)          ZorkZeroPillars    <- wrong
    shogun.cg1     58x195                  390         (0, 390) L        ShogunSinglePiece
                                                       (0, 400) R        ZorkZeroPillars    <- wrong, and the two flanks disagree

`v6_border::recognize` orders its two measurements so that reaching the native bottom wins outright (`if bottom >= native_h -> ZorkZeroPillars`), because Zork Zero's banner covers its flank from row 0 just as Shogun's border does. Shogun's DOS art is authored for the full 200-row screen rather than the Amiga's 168, so it satisfies that test too and takes Zork Zero's castle handler.

CONSEQUENCE TODAY: on a pane taller than the native aspect those flanks are extended with Zork Zero's masonry recipe — cut at unit row 86, a 284-row repeat, the bottom 26 rows stamped back as a foot — applied to Shogun's frame. Not a hole and not a stretch, so it is not the SQ-0698 symptom; it is simply the wrong recipe, and nobody has looked at it on screen.

NOT INTRODUCED BY SQ-0799 and deliberately not changed by it: SQ-0799 derives Zork Zero's four constants from the art (`pillar_shaft`), and that measurement declines on Shogun — its border is a constant-width slab with no capital wider than its shaft — so the handler keeps Bocfel's literal constants for it and Shogun's flanks are byte-identical before and after. The misclassification is older and separate.

WHAT IT PROBABLY WANTS: `recognize` needs a discriminator that is not 'reaches the bottom'. Shogun's border is one span from row 0 to its end; Zork Zero's narrows from a full-width banner to a much narrower shaft. `pillar_shaft` already computes exactly that distinction and could be promoted into the recogniser. Check the corpus guard in `crates/app/tests/suites/v6_side_border_tiling.rs` (`each_specimen_is_recognised_as_its_own_layout`) and add the DOS renditions to it, which no suite covers today.
