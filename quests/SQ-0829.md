THE SURVEY — measured 2026-08-13, do not re-derive.

`resize_directional` (`crates/app/src/render/graphics.rs:69`) is the only scaling in the app
that is BOTH direction-aware (Triangle when an axis shrinks, Nearest when it grows — SQ-0824)
and alpha-correct (premultiply across a blending pass, SQ-0827). It has exactly ONE call site:

  render/graphics.rs:1253   v6 chrome band, scaled canvas          <- the good one

Every other art-scaling site picked its own policy independently:

  render/inline_image.rs:112   FilterType::Triangle, unconditional  <- blurs pixel art on magnify
  render/screen.rs:9124        FilterType::Nearest, unconditional   <- aliases on shrink
  graphics.rs:125              Triangle via resize_exact
  graphics.rs:988              Nearest, commented "crisp DOS pixels are the house style"
  picker_ui.rs:2650            Nearest (gallery thumbnails)
  cover.rs:152 / :183          delegated to Resize::Fit(None)
  render/inline_image.rs:271   delegated to Resize::Fit(None)

TWO DEFECTS THIS IMPLIES, both already proven elsewhere in the corpus:
  - Triangle-always blurs pixel art whenever it is enlarged. SQ-0824 measured the cost on
    Journey's plate: Triangle magnification turns its 14 colours into 1636.
  - Nearest-always aliases whenever art is shrunk — that WAS the SQ-0824 defect, and at 60x24
    it dropped 54 of 222 columns entirely.
  - None but the good one premultiplies alpha, so any art with a transparent edge can bleed
    black into its border exactly as Zork Zero's flank did (SQ-0827): a transparent pixel
    carries (0,0,0), and `image::imageops::resize` filters the four channels independently.

SCOPE NOTE: this is the FILTER half only. The native-source half of SQ-0824 (sample the native
artwork once rather than a 2x replica) is v6-specific by nature — the 2x pre-doubling is what
unit space IS, and Glulx/Glk art, inline transcript images and cover art are already native.
Do not try to generalise that part.

BEWARE `graphics.rs:988`'s comment. "Crisp DOS pixels are the house style" is a deliberate
choice for MAGNIFICATION and the unified helper must preserve it — direction-awareness already
does, since Nearest is what it picks when growing. Read each site's intent before replacing it;
a site that is always magnifying loses nothing, a site that is always shrinking gains, and a
site that does both is where the bug is.

Glulx games with pixel art are the most likely visible win: shrunk through Triangle today and
magnified through Nearest, i.e. exactly backwards.

PREREQUISITE: the SQ-0824/0828 lane was asked to leave `resize_directional` REUSABLE rather
than v6-private. Check what it landed before starting.
