--- note 2026-08-12T07:26:26Z ---

USER 2026-08-12: *"for cg1 and eg1 we get a horizontal line on zork0 where we are tiling"*. Measured while answering, and the cause is arithmetic.

**Zork Zero's banner height differs per rendition; its pillar height does not.**

    id      mg1 MCGA    eg1 EGA    cg1 CGA    pic Amiga
      5       320x34     640x37     640x39     320x200     <- top banner
      6       320x39     640x39     640x39     320x200
      7       320x39     640x39     640x38     320x200
      8       320x29     640x29     640x29     320x200
    497        36x166     74x166     70x166     absent      <- left pillar
    498        37x166     74x166     72x166     absent

Pillars are **166 rows in every PC rendition**. The banner above them is **34 rows on MCGA, 37 on EGA, 39 on CGA** — so the pillars begin 3 rows lower under EGA and 5 lower under CGA.

SQ-0698's Zork Zero handler carries constants doubled from Bocfel — `extend_pillars(43, 13, 200, 142, 0, false, false)`, i.e. cut 86, unit 284, foot 26 in unit space — tuned to the layout where the banner is 34 and the pillars start at y=34 (confirmed by SQ-0795: id 497 is Amiga plate 5 cropped at (0,34)). Under EGA and CGA the cut and stride therefore land 3 and 5 rows off, and the mismatch shows as a horizontal seam.

**NEITHER LANE COULD HAVE SEEN THIS.** SQ-0698 was written and verified before SQ-0734/SQ-0790 made renditions selectable; there was exactly one Zork Zero geometry at the time, and the constants were correct for it. This is an interaction, not a defect in either piece.

**THE FIX SHAPE, and it is the more robust one anyway:** derive the pillar region from the **art actually loaded** rather than from constants pinned to one rendition — the pillar's own height is available (166 in all four), and where it starts is the banner's height, also available. Bocfel gets away with hardcoding because it only ever draws one rendition per run, chosen by display mode; babelmap lets the user switch, so our constants have to be derived where its can be fixed.

Keep the derivation honest: **measure from the art, not from a per-rendition table.** A table would work today and rot the moment a fifth source appears — and this quest exists precisely because a hardcoded layout met a source it was not written for.

**CORPUS GUARD:** Zork Zero's frames are pinned, and SQ-0698 established they did NOT move when tiling landed (band rects are unchanged by design; tiling replaces what is inside a band, never where it is). A derivation that produces the same numbers for the MCGA/Amiga path must leave them unmoved again — if a pinned frame shifts, the derivation disagrees with the constants on the case they were right about, and that is a bug in the fix.

CHECK ARTHUR AND SHOGUN TOO: both have EGA and CGA archives (`arthur.eg1`/`eg2`/`cg1`, `shogun.eg1`/`cg1`) and both are tiled by SQ-0698's handlers. If their art dimensions also vary by rendition, they have the same latent defect and it simply has not been looked at yet.

RELATED: SQ-0798 (multi-part archives — Arthur's EGA is split across `.eg1`/`.eg2`, so its tiling cannot even be judged until both parts load).

--- note 2026-08-12T08:02:46Z ---

FIXED 2026-08-12 on worktree branch `worktree-agent-a5ea3240d87d877b7` (commit 10f956b3, not merged).

DERIVED, and from where. `v6_border::pillar_shaft` reads the flank's own pixels: a pillar is a capital, a shaft and a base, and the shaft is the longest run of consecutive rows whose opaque column SPAN is identical, accepted only when that span is strictly narrower than the flank's widest painted row and something is painted above it and below it. Rows are keyed on span rather than pixel count because CGA dithers its masonry — the count wobbles row to row, the edges do not.

All four of Bocfel's castle constants then fall out of `[top, bottom)`:

    Bocfel (raw)          derived
    place_to_cut  = 43    top + INSET
    foot_height   = 13    art_bottom - bottom
    total_height  = 200   art_bottom (the caller already measured it)
    pillar_height = 142   bottom - top - 2*INSET

RESIDUE, and it is one number: INSET = 2 raw rows (4 in unit space). Bocfel keeps its repeat unit that far clear of the capital above and the base below, and that inset is the ENTIRE difference between its constants and the shaft they were cut from. Not derivable from pixels; named, commented, sourced on the constant.

MEASURED per rendition, left flank, gameplay frame at 12 blank turns:

    archive     banner  pillar drawn at  shaft (unit)  -> cut / foot / unit
    zork0.mg1   320x34      y=68          82..374         86 / 26 / 284
    zork0.eg1   640x37      y=74          88..380         92 / 20 / 284
    zork0.cg1   640x39      y=78         102..382        106 / 18 / 272
    zork0.pic   (Amiga, one full-screen plate)  82..374    86 / 26 / 284
    Zork0.blb   (Blorb)                          82..374   86 / 26 / 284

The pillar's draw row was confirmed by correlating picture 497 against the composited canvas (best match y = 68 / 74 / 78, i.e. exactly the banner's unit height), not inferred.

CORPUS GUARD HELD, byte-for-byte, two independent ways. (1) `flank_source` output hashed over 15 story/archive combinations x 3 turn counts x 2 flanks x 5 band geometries = 450 rows: exactly 60 changed, and all 60 are `zork0.eg1` and `zork0.cg1`. Nothing else moved — Zork Zero MCGA/Amiga/Blorb, all of Arthur, all of Shogun. (2) Through the REAL render path at 100x40 and 120x90, the band sources the renderer cuts are identical files before and after for `zork0.mg1`; only EGA and CGA differ.

FALSIFIED BOTH WAYS. Reverting the derivation to the constants fails the new integration case on `zork0.eg1` (first wrong row 658) and on `zork0.cg1` (first wrong row 658, 16 rows), while `zork0.mg1`, `zork0.pic` and the Blorb still pass — which is the interaction stated as a test. The unit case fails the same way at synthetic banner heights 74 and 78 and passes at 68.

TESTS. Three unit cases in `v6_border` (`the_pillar_shaft_is_measured_from_the_art_not_pinned_to_one_banner_height`, `a_constant_width_slab_declares_no_shaft`, `no_tile_boundary_repeats_the_ring_under_the_capital`) and one real-media case, `every_zork_zero_rendition_tiles_only_its_pillar_shaft` in `crates/app/tests/suites/v6_side_border_tiling.rs`, which sweeps all five Zork Zero art sources. Its oracle is independent of the fix: the shaft's span is the MODAL span of the flank's native rows, and every band row BELOW the art's own bottom, down to the base, must carry it. `boot_named` is new there — the first tier-3 boot the suite has.

ARTHUR: MEASURED, DOES NOT VARY. `arthur.mg1`/`.eg1`/`.cg1` all give banner (54) 84 raw rows and poles (170/171) 100 raw rows, i.e. identical unit geometry, and the flank art extent is (16, 384) in every one of them plus the Blorb. No latent defect, and none possible: the Arthur handler already derives its cut from `art_bottom * 9/10` rather than from a constant. The border pictures are all in `.eg1`, so this judgement does not depend on SQ-0798 — but 40 of Arthur's 137 EGA pictures still live in `.eg2` and nothing about the REST of its art can be judged until that lands.

SHOGUN: MEASURED, DOES VARY, and it is a different defect — filed as SQ-0802. Its border (picture 3) is 23x200 on MCGA, 46x200 on EGA and 58x195 on CGA against 23x168 on the Amiga floppy, so the DOS renditions reach the native screen bottom and `recognize` classifies them as ZorkZeroPillars. They have been taking Zork Zero's castle recipe since SQ-0698 and nobody has looked. This fix deliberately leaves that untouched: `pillar_shaft` declines on a constant-width slab, so those flanks keep Bocfel's literal constants and are byte-identical.
