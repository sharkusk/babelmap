USER 2026-08-12: *"for cg1 and eg1 we get a horizontal line on zork0 where we are tiling"*. Measured while answering, and the cause is arithmetic.

**Zork Zero's banner height differs per rendition; its pillar height does not.**

    id      mg1 MCGA    eg1 EGA    cg1 CGA    pic Amiga
      5       320x34     640x37     640x39     320x200     <- top banner
      6       320x39     640x39     640x39     320x200
      7       320x39     640x39     640x38     320x200
      8       320x29     640x29     640x29     320x200
    497        36x166     74x166     70x166     absent      <- left pillar
    498        37x166     74x166     72x166     absent

Pillars are **166 rows in every PC rendition**. The banner above them is **34 rows on MCGA, 37 on EGA, 39 on CGA** — so the pillars begin 3 rows lower under EGA and 5 lower under CGA.

SQ-0698's Zork Zero handler carries constants doubled from Bocfel — `extend_pillars(43, 13, 200, 142, 0, false, false)`, i.e. cut 86, unit 284, foot 26 in unit space — tuned to the layout where the banner is 34 and the pillars start at y=34 (confirmed by SQ-0795: id 497 is Amiga plate 5 cropped at (0,34)). Under EGA and CGA the cut and stride therefore land 3 and 5 rows off, and the mismatch shows as a horizontal seam.

**NEITHER LANE COULD HAVE SEEN THIS.** SQ-0698 was written and verified before SQ-0734/SQ-0790 made renditions selectable; there was exactly one Zork Zero geometry at the time, and the constants were correct for it. This is an interaction, not a defect in either piece.

**THE FIX SHAPE, and it is the more robust one anyway:** derive the pillar region from the **art actually loaded** rather than from constants pinned to one rendition — the pillar's own height is available (166 in all four), and where it starts is the banner's height, also available. Bocfel gets away with hardcoding because it only ever draws one rendition per run, chosen by display mode; babelmap lets the user switch, so our constants have to be derived where its can be fixed.

Keep the derivation honest: **measure from the art, not from a per-rendition table.** A table would work today and rot the moment a fifth source appears — and this quest exists precisely because a hardcoded layout met a source it was not written for.

**CORPUS GUARD:** Zork Zero's frames are pinned, and SQ-0698 established they did NOT move when tiling landed (band rects are unchanged by design; tiling replaces what is inside a band, never where it is). A derivation that produces the same numbers for the MCGA/Amiga path must leave them unmoved again — if a pinned frame shifts, the derivation disagrees with the constants on the case they were right about, and that is a bug in the fix.

CHECK ARTHUR AND SHOGUN TOO: both have EGA and CGA archives (`arthur.eg1`/`eg2`/`cg1`, `shogun.eg1`/`cg1`) and both are tiled by SQ-0698's handlers. If their art dimensions also vary by rendition, they have the same latent defect and it simply has not been looked at yet.
