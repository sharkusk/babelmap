--- note 2026-08-10T02:20:16Z ---

MEASURED 2026-08-09 by extracting `Pic.data` from each ADF in `stories/` with a throwaway OFS reader and comparing headers. The archives are NOT all one format:

    game      header (first 16 bytes)                              byte1  count@4  byte8 (record size)
    Zork Zero 01 06 0d 91 01 ef 00 00 0e 00 0f a7 01 00 00 0a        6      495      14
    Journey   01 06 03 b2 00 86 00 00 0e 00 78 f7 01 00 00 01        6      134      14
    Arthur    01 06 04 a7 00 a9 00 00 0e 00 7c 7c 01 00 00 1d        6      169      14
    Shogun    01 02 00 00 00 30 00 00 10 00 8a b5 01 00 00 01        2       48      16

**Shogun declares byte1 = 2 and a directory record size of 16**, where the other three declare 6 and 14. SQ-0713's reader treats record size 14 as the marker of the Amiga flavour and rejects anything else, so Shogun's entire archive is dropped and the game boots with no art — the user's "not showing any graphics at all, so no title screen".

The file itself is intact: 216832 bytes read cleanly out of the image, and forcing a 14-byte walk over it yields exactly one plausible record (id 1, 320x200) before the ids stop incrementing — the signature of reading a 16-byte layout with the wrong stride, not of a corrupt file.

WHAT TO DETERMINE, and **from the authoritative source, not by inference**: SQ-0713 got the format from Infocom's own released interpreter source (`github.com/erkyrath/infocom-zcode-terps` — prose spec at the top of `mac/gfx.c`, `EF_*` flags and `LBYTES` in `amiga/gfx.c`, palette trailer in `mac/gfx.p`). Byte 1 looks like a format VERSION and byte 8 the record size; that source should say what a version-2 record contains and whether the compression differs too. Do not assume the extra two bytes are padding and reuse the v6 field offsets.

Cross-check when it works: Shogun's Blorb (`stories/Shogun.blb`) has its own pictures, so the same oracle technique SQ-0713 used for Zork Zero — decode natively, compare byte-exact against the Blorb's PNGs for shared ids — applies here and is the right proof. Note Shogun.blb is small (191084 bytes) versus Zork Zero's 1036226, so the overlap may be partial; report the match count either way.

ALSO WORTH KNOWING for whoever does this: the user has added Beyond Zork, Zork I/II/III and Zork Undiscovered ADFs too. Those are text games and mostly carry no picture archive, but they are free coverage for the ADF mount and the story-identification rule, and worth a pass.
