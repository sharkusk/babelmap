SURVEY, measured 2026-08-13 against main `de471dc9`.

**The rule the user gave, verbatim:** "the mouse wheel should scroll the list, not move the cursor up and down. that said, the cursor should not fall off the edge of the screen as the scroll happens (e.g. the cursor should be locked to the top/bottom of the scroll window not go off screen)."

So: the wheel moves the OFFSET, and the selection is then clamped into `[offset, offset + viewport)` — it rides the edge of the window rather than being dragged along or scrolled away. Today every path does the opposite: it steps the selection with `ListScroll::move_by` (or a `*Nav` action) and `ensure_visible` drags the offset after it.

`crates/app/src/list_scroll.rs` is the natural and only home — it already owns `selected`, `offset`, `total`, and the `ScrollAnim` easing, and `ensure_visible` is already the one place the invariant lives.

**Scope, as ruled by the user: every selection list.**

  picker_ui.rs   story picker, list view      `list.move_by(d, ...)` at the coalesced-notch site (~line 716)
  picker_ui.rs   story picker, gallery view   `cover_gallery::move_index` + `list.select` — one notch = one grid ROW
  input.rs:~972  saves modal                  `Action::SavesNav(±1)`
  input.rs:~969  config screen                `Action::ConfigNav(±1)`
  input.rs:~978  file browser                 `Action::FbNav(±1)`
  ifdb_search_modal.rs                         results list

**Explicitly OUT of scope** — these are not selection lists and must not change:
  * `Action::ReplayStep` — a stepper over replay positions, not a cursor in a list.
  * The picker's preview modal — the wheel ZOOMS there (SQ-0486), deliberately.
  * The picker's info panel (`panel_scroll`) — already scrolls a body, no cursor involved.
  * `Action::BandWheel` (command band) and the debug panel — both already scroll rather than move a cursor, which is the behaviour this quest generalises.

**Two things that will bite:**

1. `picker_ui.rs` COALESCES a wheel notch: a physical notch emits several events, `pending_wheel` records the direction and one step is applied once `poll(0)` drains (so one notch = one story). Under the new rule the coalesced unit becomes one notch = one ROW of scroll, and the gallery's is one notch = one grid row. Keep the coalescing; change what it applies.
2. The offset must clamp against `total.saturating_sub(viewport)`, not `total - 1`, or the list scrolls into empty space past its end.
