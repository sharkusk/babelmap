LOCALIZED 2026-08-09 — the images are not mispositioned, they are NEVER RASTERIZED. Measured GameSession::pictures_canvas at every intro step: steps 0-5 -> [] (EMPTY), step 6 (gameplay frame) -> [win2=584x192, win7=640x400]. So no canvas is ever created for the graveyard (pic 2) or Merlin (pic 3), and the model exposes NO WinNode::Graphics at all during the intro — nothing to composite, nothing to position. This is upstream of any compositing/tiling/placement question. WHY THAT PINS IT: the draw handler in session.rs (~1549) creates the canvas UNCONDITIONALLY as its first act — 'let canvas = self.pictures_canvas.entry(ev.window).or_insert_with(|| Canvas::new(pw, ph))' — before it ever looks at whether the art resolves (the pict_source.image() fallibility is a LATER branch, and the erase branch is earlier). Therefore an empty pictures_canvas proves NO DRAW EVENT REACHED THE HANDLER for window 0 during the intro; it is not a decode failure and not a missing-art failure. Yet the opcodes definitely executed — the screen trace shows '@picture_data(2) -> h=392, w=584' and '@draw_picture(number=2, window=0, y=5, x=29)', then on the next screen a redraw of 2 plus '@draw_picture(number=3, window=0, y=51, x=81)'. NEXT STEP: find why draw_picture into WINDOW 0 does not emit/drain a picture event the way window 7 and window 2 do (they work at step 6). Look at where the VM queues the draw event vs where drain_turn applies TurnResult.pictures, and at any window-0 special case — window 0 is the story/buffer window, and session.rs also routes window-0 art to transcript-anchored inline floats (story_pics / InlineImage, the drop-cap and room-icon path, gated on is_content_art). Prime suspect: window-0 draws are being taken by that inline-float path INSTEAD of the canvas path, which is right for a drop-cap but wrong for Arthur's absolutely-placed 584x392 backdrop at (29,5). NOTE the picture sizes reported by picture_data are already UNIT space (392x584 vs raw 196x292), same as Zork0's pillars.
