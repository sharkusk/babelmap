STRONG LEAD, from SQ-0898: this is very likely the SAME RUN that quest found overwriting a character, seen from the other side of the scale.

SQ-0898's defect B was Shogun's menu rendering `START the gam` / `RESTORE a saved gam` / `QUIT the gam` on both presses below scale 1. The character was not dropped — it was OVERWRITTEN. From that quest's finding:

> the merged group is complete (`"START the game"` at col 28, 14 cells); the game's **trailing space** at native x=346 — abutting the group, covering none of it — maps to column 41 and paints over the `e`.

Same run, same native x=346, opposite symptom:

| pane | what the trailing space does |
|---|---|
| below ~scale 1 | lands ON the group's last cell and erases the `e` (SQ-0898, fixed) |
| above 85 columns | lands CLEAR of the group and paints a lone reverse-video block past it |

And the reported behaviour — *"the space between the menu item and the single reversed space grows as the number of columns grow"* — is exactly what a run positioned through the ring scale does while the group beside it advances one terminal column per character. The two rates diverge with the pane, which is the SQ-0892 group-origin mechanism (`screen.rs:2058`) restated: a run is POSITIONED through the scale but the group ADVANCES by cells, and they coincide only where a terminal column is one native 8px text cell.

So the space is being placed correctly by its own native coordinate and the group is not, or vice versa — they simply disagree, and the disagreement grows with `s`.

WHAT THIS SUGGESTS FOR THE FIX. SQ-0898 fixed the overwrite by applying SQ-0727's rule (a blank may not erase a glyph) at the one text site that lacked it. That is correct and should stay — but it addresses the *collision*, not the *placement*. This quest is the placement half: the trailing space should be part of the same group as the item it belongs to, and quantized with it, rather than positioned independently. Check whether `merge_row_fragments`/`merge_strip_fragments` (SQ-0892's "abutting only" rule, gap 0) excludes it — a trailing space that ABUTS the group ought to merge with it, and if the measured gap is non-zero at these panes that is the bug.

DO NOT simply suppress trailing blanks. The game paints them deliberately — Shogun's reverse-video selection bar is made of them, and SQ-0499/SQ-0515 both turn on reverse-video blanks being real ink. Whatever the fix is, `shogun_status_alignment` and the boot-menu selection highlight must survive it.

REPRODUCTION: both presses, `stories/shogun-r322-s890706.z6` and `stories/James Clavell's Shogun.adf`, boot menu (`wait:4500,esc,wait:2000` under `pty_capture`), any pane above 85 columns; the gap widens as columns increase. Below ~85 the same run produces SQ-0898's overwrite instead, which is now fixed — so a fix here must be checked on BOTH sides of that boundary.
