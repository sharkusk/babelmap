--- note 2026-08-22T04:02:44Z ---

CONFIRMED automatically, from two independent directions, without needing the user's eye.

1. **The app's own report.** `/dump-terminal` (SQ-0994) states it in a real pty capture of Journey r30 with kitty negotiated:

     kitty transmission compression (o=z):
       ratatui-image uploads (v6 chrome bands, the raster composite, cover + inline art): COMPRESSED — the terminal answered the o=z probe

2. **The wire.** SQ-0993's `crates/app/tests/suites/pty_kitty_compression.rs` drives a v6 story under a pty and asserts the transmits carry `o=z` with `f=32` and uncompressed `s`/`v` intact, then cross-checks that against what the report claims — their AGREEMENT being the real assertion, since either half alone could be lying.

Falsifying it by making the harness decline the probe flips the report to RAW, keeps the two in agreement, and fires the "must be ON" half — and that run measured the whole feature end to end: **4,954,086 bytes of session traffic uncompressed against 210,012 compressed, 23.6x**.

Earlier hand-measured figures stand: Journey r30 hybrid kitty payload 3,431,392 -> 67,735 B (50.7x); Zork Zero r393 raster 14,151,821 -> 159,630 B (88.7x); one 920x575 composite frame 2,821,336 -> 32,452 B (86.9x) in 5.5 ms.
