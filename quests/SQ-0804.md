MEASURED, SQ-0726.

`AppState::push_transcript_runs` opens a NEW transcript line for every turn's output. For a command turn that is right, and deliberate: an interpreter echoes a `read` along with its terminating newline (ZMSD §7.1.1.1), and `finish_command_turn` reproduces that by appending the typed command to the game's `>` prompt and letting the reply open the line below. zork1's turn output confirms it — "look" -> "West of House\nYou are standing..." with NO leading newline, and it must not run on from "There is a small mailbox here.look".

`read_char` echoes nothing at all (§10.7), so a keypress turn inherits no newline and the implicit one is invented. SQ-0726 shipped only the narrow case where the turn's whole output is ONE character (a game echoing the keystroke back), because the general form was measured and it is not safe:

Driving " \r\rnnn\r  yy\r" as keystrokes and folding EVERY non-erase read_char turn onto the previous line (`merge_line_into_previous`) against leaving it alone:

    zork0-r393-s890714.z6    0 char turns   IDENTICAL
    arthur-r74-s890714.z6    8 char turns   24 lines -> 18   DIFFERS
    shogun-r322-s890706.z6   2 char turns   19 lines -> 18   DIFFERS
    journey-r83-s890706.z6  12 char turns   29 lines -> 25   DIFFERS
    advent.z6                2 char turns   35 lines -> 34   DIFFERS
    fmvpoker.z6             12 char turns   12 lines -> 10   DIFFERS
    mysterious01.z6          4 char turns    8 lines ->  4   DIFFERS
    scopa.z6, sunburst.z6, zork1, beyondzork, amfv, borderzone, trinity — IDENTICAL

Two shapes in the DIFFERS set. Arthur is the same defect as sunburst and the fold IMPROVES it — "Please press Y or N>" + "n" become one line instead of the echo sitting below the prompt. mysterious01 is the opposite: it re-asks "Resume play on a game ?" on every invalid key with no leading newline, and the fold concatenates four of them into "Resume play on a game ?Resume play on a game ?Resume play on a game ?Resume play on a game ?". Journey/Shogun/advent/fmvpoker are menu repaints losing a blank line each.

So "continues the line" is NOT decidable from the turn's text: these games reposition between reprints (set_cursor / their own window paint) and the host transcript models none of it. The general fix needs the game's own cursor — for v6 that is `screen.v6.windows[i].x_cursor`/`y_cursor` plus the `streamed` px runs zvm already keeps, sampled before and after the turn; for v1-5 there is no screen model at all and the question may not have an answer beyond the text.

Reproducer harness: drive a session's `read_char`s, build two `AppState` transcripts side by side (plain push vs folded push) and diff them.
