## Corrected by field observation (user)

"testing with glulx and after a short delay the graphics are restored."

So the original diagnosis was half right and the conclusion was wrong. The canvases genuinely are NOT persisted — nothing in the `Glk ` snapshot carries pixels, and `pictures/` is zvm-only — but Glk's own contract covers it: `glk.rs:1235` queues non-input arrange/redraw events, and `evtype_Redraw` is delivered at the game's next `glk_select`, at which point a well-behaved game repaints its graphics windows. The "short delay" IS that round trip.

This is the same principle as the Z-machine restore discussion: the standard assumes the STORY repaints, and the interpreter's job is to ask it to. Glulx has a first-class mechanism for that (`evtype_Redraw`) which the Z-machine lacks, which is why v6 needed SQ-0588 and Glulx does not.

Residual symptom is cosmetic: the graphics window is blank from the restore until the game processes the redraw. Options if it ever matters — persist the canvases so the pre-redraw frame is not blank, or hold the previous frame until the redraw lands. Neither is worth doing on a cosmetic delay that is spec-sanctioned behaviour, so: deferred rather than closed, with the finding recorded.

NOT a blocker, and no code change made.
