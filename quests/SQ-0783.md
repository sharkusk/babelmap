FILED 2026-08-11 from the SQ-0779/SQ-0780 lane's own report, which identified these as ONE underlying cause and deliberately left both alone rather than risk regressing SQ-0750. Its words: "the SQ-0779 blank column 119 at 121x36 and the SQ-0780 `│ │` at 159x64 are both the same underlying thing — a lone glyph stamped one column wide where its native cell covers nearly two — and I left both alone deliberately rather than proving they are unfixable."

THE MECHANISM. Under SQ-0750 a lone box/block glyph the game printed is stamped **one terminal column wide, by construction** — an explicit decision taken there to avoid stamping a DOUBLE rule where the band's cell span was 2 at wide panes. But at some scales the game's native cell covers nearly two terminal columns: at 157 pane columns the game's last native text cell (native x 632..640) spans 1.96 terminal columns. Stamping it one column wide leaves the second column blank.

TWO OBSERVED SYMPTOMS, both pre-existing and both present at commit 3b21009d (before the frame work):
  1. `121x36` — a blank column 119 between the frame's right rule and babelmap's pane border, on every row.
  2. `159x64` — the menu header ends `──│ │`, a blank column between the inner rule and the outer one. Measured at EVERY width swept 150..166 (all end `──│ `), and on the top rule's `┐` and the bottom rule's `┘` as well, so it is not specific to the menu row.

WHY IT WAS NOT FIXED IN THAT LANE: widening a lone glyph to its full scaled span is precisely what SQ-0750 chose not to do, and reversing that without care reintroduces the doubled rule it was avoiding. This needs its own quest and its own falsification against BOTH regimes.

WHAT A FIX MUST SATISFY, so it does not become a see-saw with SQ-0750:
  - a lone glyph whose native cell spans ~2 terminal columns fills both, with no blank remainder at the frame's right edge;
  - a lone glyph whose native cell spans ~1 column still stamps ONE column — no doubled rule (SQ-0750's original symptom, which its tests pin);
  - sweep widths across the transition, since the span is fractional and the failure is a rounding threshold: SQ-0780's sweep found its own defect appearing at 155, vanishing at 156, returning from 157 and doubling from 164, so a single width proves nothing here;
  - pin both `honor_game_colours` modes;
  - Zork Zero, Shogun and Arthur screen-identical before and after (their side columns are artwork, not glyphs, so they should be untouched — verify rather than assume).
