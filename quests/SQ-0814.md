--- note 2026-08-12T18:18:41Z ---

FOUND 2026-08-12 by the SQ-0787 lane, reported rather than acted on so the ground fix stayed surgical.

SQ-0787 fixed ONE layer. `GameSession::paint` (SQ-0706) — the v6 screen's painted ground — rode beside the window tree, so no restore touched it and a resumed scopa came back on its main menu's cards. It now travels in the archive as `pictures/ground.png` and every restore replaces it, including with nothing.

THREE SIBLINGS ARE IN THE SAME POSITION and were left alone, all in `crates/app/src/session.rs`:

1. **`window_fills`** (`HashMap<u8, WindowFill>`, SQ-0584) — the last `erase_window` on each v6 window, what it filled and when. Read by `screen()` to give each window its background fill. Neither archived nor reset, so a restore keeps the PRE-RESTORE session's fills under the restored window table. Same shape of defect as the ground exactly. The reason it was not swept up: unlike the ground it has a bounded recipe (one small struct per window), so the right fix is probably to SERIALIZE it rather than to snapshot pixels — and clearing it blind would risk advent.z6's help panel (`erase_window` is what makes that panel opaque), which needs a test rather than a guess.

2. **`canvas_anchor`** (`HashMap<u8, CanvasAnchor>`, SQ-0715) — where each window's canvas was painted, so a later `move_window` can strand its pixels onto the ground. Per-session, not archived, not reset. A restored session inherits the previous game's anchors; the first `move_window` after a restore can then strand pixels at coordinates that belonged to a screen that no longer exists.

3. **The ground is not cleared on `@restart`.** `drain_turn`'s `just_restarted` block (session.rs, look for `std::mem::take(&mut self.machine.just_restarted)`) already clears `pictures_canvas`, `story_pics`, `last_content_pic`, `display_ops`, `unreplayable` and `window_fills` — and its own comment argues exactly why ("Nor do the pre-restart erase FILLS describe any region of the rebooted screen"). `self.paint = None` belongs in that list by the same argument and is a one-line change. It was left out only because it is a DIFFERENT trigger from the one SQ-0787 reported (restart, not restore) and no falsifying test was to hand: `apply_erase_fill` drops the ground on a full-screen clear, which most boots do, so a game has to be found that restarts without one before the fix is falsifiable.

WHERE TO START. `crates/app/tests/suites/v6_restore_paint_ground.rs` is the pattern and needs almost no adaptation — scopa (`scopa.z6`) is the fixture, since it is the story that exercises all three: it draws its whole table with fills, and it moves its scratch window between every card. The measured corpus (SQ-0787's note) says scopa, Shogun and fmvpoker are the only v6 titles that paint a ground at all; window_fills and canvas_anchor are not so narrow, so establish their corpus separately rather than inheriting that list.

CLAUDE.md's rules govern as before: perturb before asserting (the frame immediately after a restore is exactly when everything still looks correct), cover a different terminal size and a different graphics backend, and persist the recipe rather than the result — which for `window_fills` genuinely means the recipe, unlike the ground.
