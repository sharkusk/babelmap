**So Macintosh artwork exists, twice over — one archive per screen Apple sold.** That is the fixture the profile question was blocked on.

## What already works, and must not be re-litigated

`CPic.data` is an ordinary big-endian Infocom container: header `01 06 0d 3d 01 e3 …`, flags `0x06` (`HF_EHUFF | HF_GHUFF`), 14-byte records, 483 entries of which 386 carry pixels — and **all 386 decode today** through the existing `Flavour::AmigaMac` path, palettes and all. `PictSource::resolve` mounts it off the disk, `art_scale` reads its 320-wide picture space and doubles it, and `PictSource::native_std_window` supplies the 320x200 standard window (SQ-0837), so the art already lands at the right size with no profile at all. The game boots and takes input off the disk; that is committed and gated.

## The two things left, and they are separate

**1. The MONOCHROME archive does not parse.** `Pic.data` opens `01 0e 0b 5a 01 e3 …`: header flags `0x0e` and a declared directory-record size of **12**, where `InfocomPics::parse`'s Amiga/Mac path requires 14 (or 16 with per-entry trees) and returns `UnsupportedContainer`. Twelve makes sense — a two-colour screen has no palette to store — and this is exactly the file bocfel identifies by "the flags always *seem* to equal 0xe if the graphics are monochrome", the heuristic `InterpreterProfile::for_art_flavour`'s doc comment already records. Supporting it means a 12-byte record on the AmigaMac side plus whatever the mono pixel packing turns out to be; `EF_MONO` is already rejected by `decode` (`flags & (EF_MONO | EF_IFF) != 0`), so the codec work is real and not just a size constant.

**2. `InterpreterProfile::Macintosh` itself.** ZMSD §11.1.3 numbers it **3**. What is NOT established by anything in hand: the Mac's default colours, its palette, and whether a Mac v6 screen is 640x400 like the Amiga's doubled 320x200 or something else. Do not derive those from memory — that is the rule that kept them out of SQ-0837. Two candidate sources worth reading before writing a line: `mac/gfx.c` and `mac/gfx.p` in `github.com/erkyrath/infocom-zcode-terps` (Infocom's own Macintosh interpreter, already cited throughout `infocom_pics.rs`), and bocfel's Mac handling in Spatterlight. Note also that `for_art_flavour` currently maps the whole `Flavour::AmigaMac` to `Self::Amiga` and says outright "Give this a real discriminator before a Macintosh bundle is added, not after" — the monochrome `0x0e` flag is a discriminator for the B&W archive only, and the colour one on this very disk proves it does not separate colour Mac media from Amiga media. **The medium does**: a story mounted out of an HFS volume is a Macintosh, and `hints::DiskImage::Hfs` now carries that fact to every caller. That is the hook to hang the profile on, not the archive.

Until then a Mac disk resolves to `IbmPc` — pinned in `real_media_releases.rs` and in `hfs_disk_image.rs`, both of which will fail loudly when this quest changes the answer, which is the point.
