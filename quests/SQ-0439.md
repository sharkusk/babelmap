DIRECTION SETTLED with the user 2026-08-12. Read this before designing anything.

The 2026-06-23 manual-layers spec lists "Auto-derivation of layers" under Out of scope /
non-goals, and `layer.rs` says layers are "created/destroyed only by explicit peel/merge —
never derived". This quest does NOT reverse that.

CHOSEN: detect and SUGGEST, never act. The map notices a condition and PROMPTS; the player
decides. Layers still only ever come from an explicit peel, so the invariant holds literally.
Auto-peeling was considered and rejected — the reason the 06-21 auto-derived model was
reversed (in-plane placement with dotted connectors and up/down badges is good default
behaviour) still stands.

THE PROMPT offers three outcomes: **peel**, **ignore**, **mark for later**.

TWO MECHANICS THAT SHAPE EVERYTHING ELSE:
- "Ignore" MUST be remembered per-seam/region and persisted. A prompt that reappears on the
  next step is worse than no prompt at all — it would train the player to dismiss it blind.
- "Mark for later" needs somewhere to live and a way to act on it later, or it is just a
  slower "ignore".

Prompt mechanics follow the project's standing dialog conventions: shared chrome, Tab /
Shift-Tab to move focus, Enter activates, Esc cancels (Space is widget-reserved). It must not
steal focus mid-turn.

CONDITIONS: still under discussion with the user — do not implement until they are settled.

NOTE ON THE QUEST'S ORIGINAL HEURISTIC, ">2 rooms reached via in/out/up/down": it counts
portal TRAVERSALS rather than the shape of what is behind them. One `down` into a twenty-room
cellar counts as ONE and never fires — the case that most wants a layer. Three `up`s onto
three balconies counts as THREE and fires — the case that wants nothing. `peel_region` already
computes "the region on the far side of a portal seam", and reusing it means the suggestion
cannot disagree with what peeling would actually do.
