## Measured, 2026-08-20

Opening each volume of two Zork Zero releases with `pty_capture` and reading how
many bytes lanthorn emitted before exiting:

    DOS 360K set (three separate FAT12 filesystems)
      Disk 1  INSTALL.EXE, EZR.EXE, IZORK0.RUN, ZORK0.CG1     327 B — fails
      Disk 2  ZORK0.ZIP (300,032), ZORKZERO.EXE           1,052,064 B — PLAYS
      Disk 3  ZORK0.EG1                                       327 B — fails

    Apple II set (one container paged across volumes)
      zork_zero_1.dsk   opens
      zork_zero_3.dsk   opens — mounts and titles the story from a non-first volume

So the set-spanning mount answers from ANY volume for a paged container, and not at
all for a set whose volumes are independent filesystems holding distinct files. In
the DOS case only the volume that physically carries `ZORK0.ZIP` yields a story.

## Why this is worth fixing rather than explaining

**Disk 1 is the disk a player opens.** Infocom's DOS releases put the installer
there — `INSTALL.EXE` and one art rendition — and the story on disk 2. So the most
natural thing to click is the one that cannot work, and it fails with no output at
all rather than a message.

**The machinery already exists and is already proven on this exact set.**
`assets::volumes` calls `disk_set::members_indexed`, mounts the story's own volume
across the set and its siblings plainly; `read_off_the_medium` then searches all of
them. That is not theoretical here — `--pictures ZORK0.CG1` launched from Disk 2
successfully loaded the CGA art off DISK 1, the installer, and rendered Zork Zero's
whole frame in dithered 1-bit CGA. The set is recognised from these filenames and
the siblings are reachable.

What is missing is only that the STORY lookup does not ask. The story is detected
first, and the set is consulted afterwards for resources.

## Shape

When a mounted volume yields no story, consult `disk_set::members_indexed` for the
release's other volumes and look for one there before failing. Prefer the lowest
disk number that has one, so a set opens deterministically whichever member is
named.

Worth deciding: whether a story found on a sibling should report the volume the
USER named or the one it came from — `InterpreterProfile::resolve` reads the medium
from the mount, and CLAUDE.md is explicit that the medium must come from what the
mount returned rather than being re-derived from the path. Both disks here are
FAT12/DOS so the profile is the same either way, but a hybrid set could differ.

Also worth checking: the compilation guard in `assets::volumes` (`if stories > 1 {
return out; }` — "one volume of a compilation: its siblings are other games'")
exists so a Lost Treasures disc does not drag in unrelated titles. Any story-side
search needs the same guard, or opening one game's disc could open another's.

## Not a defect in what exists

The `.ima` images mount, play, license their own machine (a default-config launch
off Disk 2 gets the IBM PC without any `system_colours` opt-in) and expose three
selectable art renditions across the set — CG1 on disk 1, EG1 on disk 3, MG1 on the
720K disk 1. This quest is about reaching that from any member, not about repairing
it.
