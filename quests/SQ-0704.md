--- note 2026-08-09T06:04:26Z ---

MEASURED 2026-08-09 — the art and our canvas are both innocent; the black is introduced at final render. (1) THE ICONS ARE ~95% TRANSPARENT: pics 9/10/11/13 are the 45x40 room icons Zork0 draws into its 640x78 status window at dx=276. Measured alpha: only 77-98 of 1800 px opaque, 1702-1723 FULLY CLEAR (alpha==0), corner pixel [0,0,0,0]. They are line art on a clear ground, so whatever sits behind them is what the player sees. (Contrast pic 5, the banner, 100% opaque; and pics 479/481, the little 10x7 marks, also 100% opaque with real colours.) (2) OUR COMPOSITED CANVAS IS CORRECT: sampling the live 640x78 status canvas in gameplay, every pixel outside the icon's lit strokes is EXACTLY [0,0,0,0], and the lit pixels carry real colour ([204,136,102,255] copper, [68,0,0,255] dark red). So drawing into the canvas preserves alpha faithfully — the bug is NOT in picture decode or in compositing. (3) THEREFORE the black arrives downstream, where a transparent chrome pixel takes whatever backdrop it is drawn over. Two candidate paths: RASTER flattens holes via v6_layout::flatten_onto_page(canvas, page) where page = game_page (Zork0 sets its own) else v6_host_pair -> the 'transcript' selector (no bg) -> the OSC-probed terminal pair; HYBRID uploads the chrome band as an image and lets the terminal composite it over the CELL background, which is fill_pane_page's game page when honor_game_colours and story_bg_rgba are Some, else the theme backdrop. (4) PRIME SUSPECT: that theme backdrop was the hard-coded chrome black (Roles::terminal_default -> fg White / bg BLACK) until SQ-0510's theme half landed in 418360fc, merged minutes after this quest was filed. RETEST ON THE CURRENT BUILD BEFORE CHANGING ANYTHING — this may already be fixed. If black persists, the next measurement is which of the two paths is live (/dump-windows now reports the render path) and what fill_pane_page/story_bg_rgba actually resolve for Zork0.

--- note 2026-08-09T06:06:53Z ---

ROOT CAUSE FOUND 2026-08-09 (retest confirmed still black after 418360fc, so NOT the chrome/terminal-background bug). The icons live in win1, Zork0's 640x78 BANNER window — so the relevant background is WIN1's own, not the story window's. build_chrome_canvas (v6_layout.rs:557) takes a SINGLE default_fg/default_bg pair for ALL chrome windows; a window's own colour pair is consulted only for text-run backgrounds (fill_explicit_bg_rows, SQ-0519), never to fill the window's own rect, and the Graphics pass blits with alpha untouched and no window-background fill at all. So win1's white page is never painted and the ~95%-transparent icons fall through to the global page/theme backdrop. ZMSD 8.8.3.2 is explicit that this is wrong: each Version 6 window has its OWN foreground/background pair (property 11) - verified by fetch this session, alongside 8.8.3.1 (buffering = window attribute 3) and 8.8.3.2.3 (set_text_style sets the CURRENT WINDOW's style). Scope widened by the user: verify transparent-background handling across ALL engines, not just v6 - Glulx Glk windows carry their own bg (GridWindow.bg, Pair.key_bg/key_fg) and Scott has its picture band over a room panel.
