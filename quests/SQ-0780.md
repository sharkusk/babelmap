TWO gaps in the one row, and they may be the same fault seen twice:
 1. after the `Individual Commands` label, the horizontal rule begins one column late, leaving a blank cell between the label's last character and the rule's first;
 2. at the row's right end, `│ │` — a blank column between the inner rule and the outer one.

Note `The Party`'s rule does NOT show the defect at this width — it abuts its label on both sides — so whatever it is, it is not symmetric across the two labels. That asymmetry is the best lead: compare how the two labels' spans are computed.

WHERE TO LOOK: `collapse_row_rules` in `crates/app/src/render/screen.rs` and its `merge_row_fragments(&pending, 4)` field-gap distinction. This is the same function SQ-0742's second pass changed ("a lone line-drawing glyph is a divider, and stands in its own column") and whose rule-start behaviour is described there as "a rule's left edge is the previous span's END". If a label's end column is computed from its scaled native pixel while the rule's start is computed from a cell boundary, the two disagree by one cell at some widths — the ceil-vs-round-on-a-shared-boundary trap CLAUDE.md names for this whole area.

MEASURE FIRST: sweep widths to find the exact onset. The user says "starting at 159", so capture 155..165 and find the first width that shows it; whether the gap then persists at every larger width or comes and goes will distinguish a rounding threshold from an off-by-one in a divisor. SQ-0742's lineage has repeatedly produced defects present at 83% of widths and absent at the rest, so a single width proves very little either way.
