REPRODUCED ON THE REAL ZORK MAP (~/.babelmap/saves/zork1-invclues-r52-s871125.z5.save/default.babelmap, relayout_auto, layer 0):

  68->143 (NE) diag_cells: [(11,25)'🮠', (55,20)'🮣', (55,21)'│', (55,22)'🮠', (54,22)'🮣', (54,23)'🮠', (53,23)'🮣']
  28->195 (Up) cells:      [(55,22) mask=10, (54,22) mask=10]   <- mask 10 = E|W, a horizontal run

Both want (54,22) and (55,22). Up/Down connectors accumulate into a SEPARATE per-cell mask (updown_cells) and paint with the DOTTED portal glyphs; that paints last and wins, so the chain silently loses two cells and the diagonal reads as broken where the dotted line crosses it. Visible in the render as a continuous `└┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┐` with the chain resuming below it.

SURVEY of the whole Zork map (34 chain cells total):
  chain-vs-chain collisions:      0
  chain-vs-orthogonal collisions: 2  (the pair above)
So chain-vs-chain may not be reachable in practice; chain-vs-line is, and is the case worth solving.

WHY THIS IS NOT A MECHANICAL FIX — it needs a design decision, which is why it is filed rather than fixed:
There is no box-drawing glyph for "diagonal crosses horizontal". Something must give. Options, none obviously right:
  (a) the crossing line wins and the chain accepts a gap (today's accidental behaviour — reads as a broken diagonal);
  (b) the chain wins and the crossed line takes the gap (a dotted portal line with a hole);
  (c) route around: teach the router that a chain occupies cells, so tidy/lane assignment can avoid the crossing;
  (d) pick a crossing glyph per case (no clean candidate exists for diagonal-over-line).

RELATED, AND THE REASON THIS WENT UNNOTICED: overlap_stats/connector_ownership score the ORTHOGONAL reading only (plot_connector(.., None)), deliberately, so the tidy metric cannot move when the user toggles the `diagonal_corners` DISPLAY setting. Chain cells are therefore invisible to the metric, and tidy can never nudge a room to fix a chain collision it cannot see. Making the metric chain-aware means choosing ONE geometry to score (the occupied cells genuinely differ between the toggle's two settings), which is the same design decision as above.

Current overlap_stats on the Zork map reports illegal=3 total=7 — none of them these two cells.
