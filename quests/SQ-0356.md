  28->195 (Up) cells:      [(55,22) mask=10, (54,22) mask=10]   <- mask 10 = E|W, a horizontal run

Both want (54,22) and (55,22). Up/Down connectors accumulate into a SEPARATE per-cell mask (updown_cells) and paint with the DOTTED portal glyphs; that paints last and wins, so the chain silently loses two cells and the diagonal reads as broken where the dotted line crosses it. Visible in the render as a continuous `└┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┐` with the chain resuming below it.

SURVEY of the whole Zork map (34 chain cells total):
  chain-vs-chain collisions:      0
  chain-vs-orthogonal collisions: 2  (the pair above)
So chain-vs-chain may not be reachable in practice; chain-vs-line is, and is the case worth solving.

WHY THIS IS NOT A MECHANICAL FIX — it needs a design decision, which is why it is filed rather than fixed:
There is no box-drawing glyph for "diagonal crosses horizontal". Something must give. Options, none obviously right:
  (a) the crossing line wins and the chain accepts a gap (today's accidental behaviour — reads as a broken diagonal);
  (b) the chain wins and the crossed line takes the gap (a dotted portal line with a hole);
  (c) route around: teach the router that a chain occupies cells, so tidy/lane assignment can avoid the crossing;
  (d) pick a crossing glyph per case (no clean candidate exists for diagonal-over-line).

RELATED, AND THE REASON THIS WENT UNNOTICED: overlap_stats/connector_ownership score the ORTHOGONAL reading only (plot_connector(.., None)), deliberately, so the tidy metric cannot move when the user toggles the `diagonal_corners` DISPLAY setting. Chain cells are therefore invisible to the metric, and tidy can never nudge a room to fix a chain collision it cannot see. Making the metric chain-aware means choosing ONE geometry to score (the occupied cells genuinely differ between the toggle's two settings), which is the same design decision as above.

Current overlap_stats on the Zork map reports illegal=3 total=7 — none of them these two cells.

--- note 2026-07-16T00:27:01Z ---

Resolved as option C (tee junction), chosen by the user from four renders of the real collision.

Probing the CURRENT Zork map corrected this quest's premise: there is exactly ONE colliding cell map-wide (35 chain cells vs 624 line cells), and it is not a chain crossing an unrelated line. It is #68 and #143 fighting over their own shared lane — `68 -NE-> 143` (reciprocal with `143 -SW-> 68`, collapsed to one diagonal) and a SECOND distorted `143 -W-> 68` edge route down the same column, and the diagonal's last chain cell lands on the W route's vertical. The earlier note's "2 chain-vs-orthogonal, up/down connectors win" reading did not survive re-measurement; no chain/up-down collision exists.

Fix: every half-diagonal endpoint is an edge MIDPOINT — exactly where ─ and │ attach — so a chain glyph and an orthogonal run through one cell describe strokes to the SAME points and can merge into one mask. `chain_glyph_bits` reads a chain glyph's two endpoint bits (themeable glyphs, matched against PathGlyphs, not literals); a chain cell landing on another connector's compass run ORs those bits into the shared mask instead of painting over it, so the cell renders as the junction carrying both strokes (│ + E|S -> ├). Merging via the mask rather than by painting is what makes it order-independent: a connector painting the cell later ORs on top and the chain's bits survive.

Scope kept tight: up/down connectors are excluded (separate dotted mask — a chain has nothing there to merge WITH), and pure-diagonal cells still paint the raw half-diagonal. A genuine chain crossing an unrelated perpendicular line still has no glyph and would still need A-vs-B; that case occurs nowhere on the map, so it is not built for.

Tests: `a_chain_cell_on_another_connectors_run_merges_into_a_junction` (real #68/#143 geometry, collision derived from the plots rather than hard-coded) and `chain_glyph_bits_name_each_half_diagonals_own_endpoints`. Mutants die: never-merge fails with the reported `│`; merge-unconditionally breaks two existing diagonal tests. No new clippy warnings (25 before and after).
