--- note 2026-08-14T04:38:57Z ---

Widened by SQ-0836's findings: the segmented Apple II story container is NOT only a 5.25-inch problem. `Arthur Quest 4 Excalibur.2mg` and `Journey.2mg` are 800K ProDOS volumes that mount perfectly and contain NO whole story file — the Apple II press splits each game across `ARTHUR.1/ARTHUR.D1`…`.D5` and `JOURNEY.D1`…, and none of those parts begins with a Z-header. (A raw byte scan finds a header partway INSIDE `.D1` — at offset 37 970 for Arthur, 4 690 for Journey — which is what fooled an earlier probe of mine into reporting them as releases r63/s890622 and r77/s890616. Neither is a whole story; treat both numbers as retracted.)

So this quest is really "read Infocom's segmented Apple II story container", and it has two carriers rather than one: the `.D1`–`.D5` files inside a ProDOS volume, and the filesystem-less 140K `.dsk` sets (Shogun s1–s5, Zork Zero 1–4) where the same splitting happens with no directory at all. Solving the container solves both; solving only the `.dsk` sector layout leaves the IIgs v6 games unreachable.

Both `.2mg` disks currently mount, list their files and correctly report no game, so nothing is broken — the games are simply not yet readable.
