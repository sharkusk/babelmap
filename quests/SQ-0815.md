MEASURED — the premise is falsified: the blend DOES reach the side art.

Measured on stories/zork0-r393-s890714.z6 with pictures = "zork0.eg1", against zork0.mg1, on the window-7 border canvas in unit space (640x400), story window at (86,78) 468x320 so the flanks are columns 0..86 and 554..640.

Flank horizontal speckle, raw vs fused:
  left  62.86 -> 12.74      right 62.17 -> 12.45   (MCGA flanks 6.05 / 5.68)
Folded into the 320-wide space both artists drew in (EGA averaged in pairs, MCGA de-doubled), which is the only like-for-like comparison:
  left  25.46 -> 17.48      MCGA 12.32

No path bypasses the filter. Checked and cleared: PictSource::from_native is the only constructor and native_image is the ONLY place an archive becomes an image in the whole app (both PictSource::image paths pass blend_columns); v6_layout::build_chrome_canvas carries the fused pixels (flank 14.98 vs MCGA 7.66, i.e. matched once MCGA's doubled columns are accounted for); build_v6_raster_canvas after extend_raster_flanks likewise (14.59/13.24); v6_border::flank_source -> zork_zero -> extend_pillars -> tile_down(flip) copies canvas pixels, so SQ-0808's mirror cannot re-separate a fused pair (a vertical mirror does not touch the horizontal axis) and there is no tile seam to fuse. Every native archive in stories/ is eligible and gated correctly (arthur/journey/shogun/zork0 .eg1 all art_scale (1,2) mono=false; all three .cg1 mono=true).

WHY IT STILL SPECKLES. The tent is a NOTCH, not a low-pass: [1,2,1]/4 has response cos^2(pi f), which is exactly zero at period 2 and 0.5 at period 4. The boot frame's arch is a clean period-2 two-colour dither, so it fuses perfectly — the frame's flat interior comes out at horizontal speckle 0.00. Zork Zero's PILLARS are error-diffusion dithered instead: seven EGA entries (both greys, bright red, yellow, brown, dark red, black) in irregular runs, the signature of an automatic colour reducer on a smooth bronze gradient. Broadband noise has energy at every frequency and a three-tap kernel removes only the top of it.

WHY THE KERNEL WAS NOT WIDENED. It works — [1,2,2,2,1]/8 = box2 (x) box4, zeros at BOTH period 2 and period 4, i.e. exactly the detail a 320-wide plate cannot carry:
  filter      flank L   whole speckle   whole dist to MCGA
  none         62.86        49.12            44.51
  box2         17.93        11.45            29.65
  tent 121     12.74         8.40            27.79   <- shipped
  1,3,3,1      10.40         6.94            27.17
  1,4,6,4,1     8.86         6.01            26.55
  1,2,2,2,1     6.98         4.80            26.04   (MCGA flank 6.05, MCGA whole 4.33)
Every number improves. It was still rejected, because the SAME frame carries the compass rose, whose N/W/E/S are 640-wide line art the card resolved perfectly well — rendered at 3x, they are crisp raw, legible under the tent, and smudges under [1,2,2,2,1]. [1,3,3,1] keeps the letters but only reaches 10.40, which is not worth a filter change. One plate, two kinds of detail at the same frequency, and no linear filter tells them apart.

So: no code change for part one. The trade is documented in docs/features/v6-graphics.md ("Where the fusing stops, and why it stays there") with the numbers, and SQ-0816's fuse_art_dither switch lets anyone who disagrees turn the whole thing off. Part two (the stale caveat) IS fixed.
