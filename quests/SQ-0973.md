--- note 2026-08-21T20:16:42Z ---

CONFIRMED by the user: "v6-raster mode with the new half-block changes is FAST". Closing.

Measured before/after on the merged branch (release, real frames, booted through the full startup.rs chain): at a 458x144 pane the encode went 155-164 ms / 50.00 MB intermediate -> 2.23-2.34 ms / 0.50 MB, a 70x improvement; 28x at 240x80, 20x at 200x60, 8x at 100x40.

Two things worth carrying forward from this quest:

1. The intermediate was 50 MB, not the ~4.8 MB predicted when the quest was filed. `Picker::halfblocks()` reports a NOMINAL 10x20 font whatever the terminal's real one is, so the device box at 458x144 was 4580x2880 and the free scale 7.16x, not 2.16x. Any future reasoning about half-blocks geometry must use the nominal font size, not the real one.

2. The pixel-lock path was THREE resamples, not two — `Resize::Fit`'s `needs_resize` returns Some in the locked branch and re-resizes and pads on top of the pre-scale. Only the free-magnify branch was the two-resample case described when the quest was filed.

Follow-ons, both recorded with their dependencies rather than left hanging: SQ-0978 (the pixel lock quantizes in device pixels, which a cell-resolution backend does not have) and SQ-0979 (the same double resample still serves cover art, gallery tiles, the resource preview and the Glulx graphics-window blit).
