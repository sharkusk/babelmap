WHY THIS EXISTS — SQ-0841's root cause, which is the exact shape of defect this is meant to catch.

`v6_border::recognize` asked whether the art reached the native screen bottom **exactly**. A v6 screen is the archive's picture space rounded up to a whole 8x16 cell. The Macintosh mono space is 480x300, so the screen is **304** rows and pillars painted to 300 missed it by four pixels — the flank was misclassified, a handler stamped a mirrored copy of the whole column below the first, and the foot ended up stranded mid-column with bare shaft beneath it.

**Every other rendition divides exactly, so that test had never been wrong in its life.** It was found by eye, in one room, on one border set, on one newly-supported format. Nothing would have caught it, and nothing would catch its siblings.

## The design, which the user asked for

The composition is a PURE FUNCTION of the art and the target height, and `crates/app/tests/suites/v6_mac_pillar_feet.rs` already drives `v6_border::flank_source` and `recognize` directly at four pane heights. The only thing tying it to one room is that it takes the flank art from a live `GameSession`'s current screen.

So: **take the art from the ARCHIVE instead.**

1. Decode every picture in each available archive (`InfocomPics` reaches all of them by id).
2. For each picture `recognize` accepts as a flank, run `flank_source` at several pane heights.
3. Assert the composition's properties at each height.

This is **self-discovering** — it finds the vines, the ice, and every other border variant without anyone knowing their ids, which matters because the ids could not be identified from the directory. Coverage is every border set in the corpus rather than the rooms someone happened to walk to.

## Formats to sweep — the user: "we should probably do this for our other formats as well"

Macintosh mono (`Pic.data`) and colour (`CPic.data`) off `stories/Zork Zero Disk.image`; the Amiga archives inside the `.adf` floppies; the PC archives as loose files in `stories/` (`.MG1` MCGA, `.EG1`/`.EG2` EGA, `.CG1` CGA). Games with border art: Zork Zero, Arthur, Journey, Shogun. Each fixture must skip vacuously when absent — `stories/` is gitignored.

The Macintosh mono archive holds **all 483 ids**, two of which (444, 478) carry art the colour archive does not.

## Nuances that will bite

* **`recognize` takes a SCREEN REGION, not a bare picture** — `(&gfx, x0, x1, art, native_h)`. Each candidate has to be painted into a synthetic frame at a flank's x-range first. A helper, not a blocker; `v6_mac_pillar_feet.rs`'s `Flank` scaffolding is most of it.
* **Not every flank has a foot.** `tile_down`'s own doc: "Arthur needs neither (his repeat unit is two lines of a plain texture); Zork Zero's patterned masonry is the case they were written for." So "foot on the last row" holds only where the art HAS a base. State the properties so they are universally true — *the composition fills exactly to the target height* and *strides are uniform* hold everywhere; the foot assertion is conditional on the classification.
* **Record the classification each picture gets.** That inventory is half the value: it says which arm of `recognize` each border set exercises, and it is what tells a future reader whether a new format has art no arm handles.
* **Keep it bounded.** Hundreds of pictures times several heights times several archives can get slow; the workspace gate is ~100s and should stay near it. Sample heights rather than sweeping them, and say in the report what was and was not covered — a silent cap reads as "we checked everything".
