Both reported by the user against merged main `70fa7269`. Both MEASURED and reproduced below. Both are clean at 100x40 / 8x18 — which is why neither the SQ-0894 nor the SQ-0892 lane caught them, and why any fix must be swept across pane sizes rather than checked at one.

## Defect A — Arthur's banner corners are squeezed (REGRESSION, from SQ-0894 step (a), commit c01dc724)

The content-derived flank ROWS let a flank extend up over the banner, so Arthur's banner is drawn in three pieces: `flank | banner | flank`. At 100x40 the numbers happen to agree and it looks right. At the user's pane they do not.

MEASURED, `arthur-r74-s890714.z6`, pane 78x49:

    band 4x10@(1,1)              native 37x192@(0,0)   resample 640x400->608x380
    band 4x35@(1,12) [Art,tiled] source 34x663         resample 34x663->32x630

| piece | native width | device width | horizontal magnification |
|---|---|---|---|
| flank over the banner | 37 px | 4 cells = 32 px | **0.865** |
| the banner itself | — | — | **0.950** |
| the pole below it | 34 px | 32 px | **0.941** |

Three magnifications on one column. The banner-row flank piece crops 37 native px into a 32 px destination — a 9% squeeze — and that is the visible artefact: a fragment of banner pattern at each top corner that does not line up with the banner beside it.

At 100x40: `native 33x176` into 5 cells = 40 px, and 33 x 1.225 = 40.4. Agreement by luck, no artefact.

Reproduces at 78x49, 76x50, 80x50, 78x45, 82x48. The user reports it on the Amiga, DOS and Macintosh b/w presses.

## Defect B — Shogun's menu loses its final character

`START the game` renders as `START the gam`, likewise `RESTORE a saved gam`, `QUIT the gam`, on BOTH presses.

MEASURED off the emitted stream: at 78x49, 76x50 and 78x45 the CELL BUFFER ITSELF is truncated — this is not a placement drawing over the text. At 100x40, 120x40, 140x50, 163x61, 171x50, 200x60 and at seven cell metrics (6x14 through 12x24) it is complete. The oracle reports only the two flank placements at cols 1..8 / 91..98 and both decoders agree, so nothing composites over those cells.

INFERRED, and the lane must confirm it: the menu group is positioned through the ring scale but ADVANCES ONE TERMINAL COLUMN PER CHARACTER (this is stated in the SQ-0892 comment at `screen.rs:2058`). The two rates coincide only where a terminal column is one native 8px text cell. At s = 0.95, `START the game` is 14 chars = 112 native px = 106 device px = 13.3 cells, but the group advances 14 columns — so the last column falls outside whatever bound clips it and the character is dropped. Candidate clip: `screen.rs:5719`, `if c >= rect.x as i32 && c < rect.right() as i32`.

## THE UNIFYING DIAGNOSIS

Both are the same class: **a piece's NATIVE extent and its CELL extent are computed independently, and they disagree once the letterbox scale is not ~1.0.** One squeezes art, the other drops a character. CLAUDE.md names this class directly — "v6 geometry bugs are usually cell-quantization issues (art scaled by pixel, text placed by cell — watch for ceil-vs-round mismatches on shared boundaries)".

## THE USER'S STEER on defect A

Verbatim: *"it's okay to scale the poles up at the same ratio as the banner"* and *"likely preferred to get them to properly match"*.

So a single magnification down the whole column is acceptable and matching exactly is preferred. That is the same principle SQ-0894 established for the Zork Zero seam — one column, one source, one scale — applied to a column whose art changes partway down.

Also from the user: *"it works properly at a height of 22. going smaller actually has a slightly different problem."* Get the exact metric from them if the sweep does not explain it; do not assume which "height" is meant.

## DO NOT

- Do not fix defect A by forbidding a flank from extending over the banner. That would undo SQ-0894's Zork Zero seam fix, where the flank spanning the banner rows continuously IS the fix (one source `91x544`, one resample `91x544->112x666`). The problem is the SCALE disagreement, not the extension.
- Do not regress the SQ-0892 acceptance: both Shogun credits screens on the ring, crisp glyphs, panels full height, `You may choose to:` beside the menu, and Zork Zero's banner still `strip:art 70x6 at (15,1)`.
