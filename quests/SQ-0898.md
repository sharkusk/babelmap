## Defect B — Shogun's menu loses its final character

`START the game` renders as `START the gam`, likewise `RESTORE a saved gam`, `QUIT the gam`, on BOTH presses.

MEASURED off the emitted stream: at 78x49, 76x50 and 78x45 the CELL BUFFER ITSELF is truncated — this is not a placement drawing over the text. At 100x40, 120x40, 140x50, 163x61, 171x50, 200x60 and at seven cell metrics (6x14 through 12x24) it is complete. The oracle reports only the two flank placements at cols 1..8 / 91..98 and both decoders agree, so nothing composites over those cells.

INFERRED, and the lane must confirm it: the menu group is positioned through the ring scale but ADVANCES ONE TERMINAL COLUMN PER CHARACTER (this is stated in the SQ-0892 comment at `screen.rs:2058`). The two rates coincide only where a terminal column is one native 8px text cell. At s = 0.95, `START the game` is 14 chars = 112 native px = 106 device px = 13.3 cells, but the group advances 14 columns — so the last column falls outside whatever bound clips it and the character is dropped. Candidate clip: `screen.rs:5719`, `if c >= rect.x as i32 && c < rect.right() as i32`.

## THE UNIFYING DIAGNOSIS

Both are the same class: **a piece's NATIVE extent and its CELL extent are computed independently, and they disagree once the letterbox scale is not ~1.0.** One squeezes art, the other drops a character. CLAUDE.md names this class directly — "v6 geometry bugs are usually cell-quantization issues (art scaled by pixel, text placed by cell — watch for ceil-vs-round mismatches on shared boundaries)".

## THE USER'S STEER on defect A

Verbatim: *"it's okay to scale the poles up at the same ratio as the banner"* and *"likely preferred to get them to properly match"*.

So a single magnification down the whole column is acceptable and matching exactly is preferred. That is the same principle SQ-0894 established for the Zork Zero seam — one column, one source, one scale — applied to a column whose art changes partway down.

Also from the user: *"it works properly at a height of 22. going smaller actually has a slightly different problem."* Get the exact metric from them if the sweep does not explain it; do not assume which "height" is meant.

## DO NOT

- Do not fix defect A by forbidding a flank from extending over the banner. That would undo SQ-0894's Zork Zero seam fix, where the flank spanning the banner rows continuously IS the fix (one source `91x544`, one resample `91x544->112x666`). The problem is the SCALE disagreement, not the extension.
- Do not regress the SQ-0892 acceptance: both Shogun credits screens on the ring, crisp glyphs, panels full height, `You may choose to:` beside the menu, and Zork Zero's banner still `strip:art 70x6 at (15,1)`.

## The root cause, one sentence, and it IS shared

A piece's NATIVE extent and its DEVICE/CELL extent are computed independently, and where one of them is clipped the other is not — so the resize (defect A) or the neighbouring run (defect B) absorbs the difference. Both are invisible at or above scale 1 and both appear below it.

## Defect A — Arthur's corners. The tiled flank, and ONLY the tiled flank.

`flank_native_box` clipped the source to the artwork that exists (`.max(0.0)`, `.min(native)`) — there are no native columns outside the game's screen — and `draw_chrome_band_image` resized whatever it was handed into the WHOLE band. Nothing reconciled them.

MEASURED, `arthur-r74-s890714.z6`, reproduced exactly from the user's own dump:

    pane 70x19, scale 0.8550, off (6,0)
    before  band 4x9@(0,10) [Art,tiled]  source 30x189 -> 32x162   1.0667/0.8571
    after   band 4x9@(0,10) [Art,tiled]  source 30x189 -> 26x162 at (6,0) of 32x162   0.8667/0.8571

    right pole, before 29x189 -> 32x162 (1.1034);  after -> 25x162 at (0,0)

The pole was also six device px LEFT of the crop above it, because the destination started at the band's edge while the crop started at the letterbox margin. That is the corner fragment.

## THE CROP BANDS WERE NEVER AT FAULT — correcting the analysis in this quest's own note

The 0.865/0.800/1.13x figures in the note above, and the "native x 28..33 overlap", are **artefacts of the band log**, not of the render. The log's `native WxH@(x,y)` on a crop is the SQ-0514 hash FOOTPRINT and it carries `scale_halo`'s area-filter halo — `ceil(1/s)+1`, which is 0 at s>=1 and 3 at s=0.855. Verified by arithmetic on the user's own 70x19 log: the bands read scaled-canvas pixels 0..26, 26..90, 90..154, … a exact partition of the 547px canvas, every one at 0.855. A crop copies device pixels 1:1 out of one image that was resized once; it cannot be anisotropic and cannot disagree with its neighbours. Two of this suite's own cases were computing factors from that field and are retargeted.

At the user's 78x49 (pane 76x46, off_x = 0) the flank's residual was |32 - 34x0.95| = 0.3 device px — real but sub-pixel. **A horizontal letterbox margin (`off_x > 0`) is what makes it visible, and that needs a pane wider than 640/400 of its height.** 70x19 has it, 78x49 does not.

## Defect B — Shogun's menu. NOT a dropped character; an overwritten one.

The merged group IS complete. Probed at pane 76x46:

    run "START the game"  col=28 row=18 max_w=42     <- 14 chars, cols 28..41
    run " "               col=41 row=18              <- the game's trailing space, INSIDE it

`RESTORE a saved game` at 28..47 with its blank at 47; `QUIT the game` at 28..40 with its blank at 40. Every item loses its last letter to a space, which is why the loss was one character regardless of length and why `max_w`/the viewport clip was never involved.

The group takes 14 columns from native x=234; the blank at native x=346 abuts it exactly (234 + 14x8 = 346) and covers none of it, but maps through the scale to column 41. Fixed with SQ-0727's own rule at the one text site that never got it: a blank paints only the cells no glyph run on that row claimed, cell by cell so a selection bar still reaches past its label.

## THE GATE — the real deliverable

`GraphicsRender::band_mags`: per band, `(rect, BandFit, source native WxH, destination device WxH)`, recorded from the sizes that go INTO the resample. `v6_side_border_tiling::every_band_draws_at_the_frames_one_magnification` asserts every `Letterbox` band lands within ONE NATIVE PIXEL of the frame's letterbox scale on both axes, across 3 specimens x 6 panes. `BandFit::Fitted` names the two deliberate exceptions: the Menu-plan flank PANEL (quantized to whole cells by `aspect_cells`, SQ-0547) and the divider EXTENSION (one native row replicated, SQ-0511).

## The defect, and why the first round's gate could not see it

`screen.rs`'s `matches!(plan, BottomPlan::Frame) && role.is_flank()` arm — `flank_crop` + `draw_chrome_band_stretched` — drew Arthur's banner-row pole at **0.63 device px per native px against the frame's 1.35**. Both top corners showed his whole pole squashed into the banner's height while the pole below rendered correctly.

`every_band_draws_at_the_frames_one_magnification` (added last round) exempted every band tagged `BandFit::Fitted`, and `Fitted` was recorded INSIDE `draw_chrome_band_stretched` as a constant. **The exemption therefore belonged to the DRAWING FUNCTION, so every caller of it was outside the gate by construction.** Two callers were the intended exceptions (SQ-0547 Menu panel, SQ-0511 divider extension); the third was this arm, which inherited theirs in silence. `BandFit` is now `Letterbox | MenuPanel | DividerExtension` and the caller declares its own, so an unnamed site is held to the frame's scale.

## SQ-0894's "kept, unfalsifiable" decision — falsified, with the measurement it asked for

That lane recorded: disabling the arm passes the full gate, which is evidence the corpus has no fixture, not that the arm is dead; it fires only for a flank `recognize` does NOT know, and every shipped flank is recognised.

**Half right. `flank_source` declines for TWO reasons and only one was looked for.** "Unrecognised" is the one no shipped title has. The other is `desired <= art.1` — the band lies wholly INSIDE the artwork and needs no extension at all. Arthur reaches it on BOTH presses at EVERY pane swept, for the one turn his story window grows to `(28, 208, 584, 192)` (bottom 400) and selects `BottomPlan::Frame`; his 72-run status bar cuts the pole in two and the piece above it is entirely within the art.

**No correct use remains, and that is a statement about the arm rather than about the corpus.** It can only fire where extension is impossible (nothing recognised to extend) or unnecessary (the art already covers the band). The first invents rows by distorting the ones there are; the second distorts rows that were already complete. Both fall to the plain crop, which is the frame's own magnification by construction — so removing it cannot leave a frame open that the arm would have closed. `flank_crop` and `flank_native_bottom` went with it.
