## ANSWERED — the RESOLVED pair (#0000AD/#ADADAD) is authoritative; the stored constant is dead and must go

Two reasons, and the second decides it on its own.

**1. Consistency, or the screen shows a seam.** `period_look_for` resolves `default_colours` through `ega_true_colour` -> `rgb15_to_888`, which is the SAME path a story's own §8.3.1 colour request takes. If a story sets its background to colour 6, lanthorn paints #0000AD. Had the default page been painted #0000AA, the two would abut at a window edge — two blues three parts in 255 apart. Whatever lanthorn paints by default MUST equal what the story's colour numbers resolve to.

**2. The stored value cannot be correct in principle.** The IBM PC's pair is VERSION-DEPENDENT: XZIP resolves colour 9 to #ADADAD, YZIP to #FFFFFF (that is the one entry the two Infocom interpreters disagree on, and v6 selects YZIP). A single stored constant cannot represent a quantity that differs by story Version. That is exactly why `period_look_for` derives it — and why the field is not merely stale for row 6, it is unrepresentable.

Verified: the overwrite at `crates/zvm/src/interpreter.rs:945` fires only for `Palette::IbmXzip | Palette::IbmYzip`. Every other machine keeps its stored pair, so this is the IBM PC's row alone.

## What to do

- **Remove the dead pair from the IBM PC's stored `period_look`**, and prefer a shape that makes the mistake unrepeatable rather than just correcting the numbers — the point is that a machine whose look is derived should not be able to carry a second, contradictory answer. If that means the field becomes optional, or the IBM row is constructed differently, that is a better outcome than a corrected constant nobody reads.
- **Update `docs/features/interpreter.md`'s period-look table** so row 6 states what the screen shows. The table is stated as the v1-v5 look, so that is XZIP: page #0000AD, ink #ADADAD.
- **Do NOT delete #0000AA/#AAAAAA from the record.** They are the truth about the EGA hardware, and the 15-bit round-trip (0xAA -> 21/31 -> 0xAD) is a Z-machine artifact, not an error in either value. Keep it as a note beside the row; SQ-0960 already wrote that explanation into the `--machines` legend and the doc, so match that wording rather than inventing a second one.
- The machine capture cannot adjudicate: `machine-screenshots/dos-hitchhiker.png` shows a scaled #0F009E, nowhere near 3/255 precision on either candidate.
