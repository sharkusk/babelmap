## The gap, stated exactly

Hybrid's story viewport is CELLS ONLY. Step 29 of the pipeline (`render_node(&story.node, …, viewport, …)`, `screen.rs`) renders the transcript as terminal text and nothing else. There is no way to put a pixel inside that box.

That single fact is why `picture_takeover` exists. Its four hatches (`screen.rs:4035-4040`) are four spellings of one sentence — *"there are pixels here the ring has nowhere to put"* — and their answer is to hand the entire frame to the raster composite:

    story_covers_screen && (art_paints_anything || art_fills_screen || art_encloses_screen)
    || story_plate_escapes_story_window

## Why this is the LAST capability gap, not one of several

MEASURED during SQ-0894 (2026-08-15): hybrid already handles text-over-art everywhere EXCEPT inside the story box. Zork Zero's banner — text printed onto the ribbon artwork — is classified as an Art strip by `over_art` and rasterised into the band image, and that works on every backend including half-blocks, where a "band image" is drawn as `▀` cells.

So the often-repeated claim that raster must survive for protocol reasons is WRONG, and I made it myself earlier in SQ-0894 before checking. Half-blocks and sixel cannot show a glyph and artwork in the same CELL — but hybrid never asks them to. It rasterises the strip. The capability is already there; it is simply unavailable in one region.

**Give the story viewport the same treatment the chrome bands already get, and "raster mode" becomes "hybrid whose story region happens to be one full-screen Art strip".**

## The governing rule is already written, and it is not "is this pixel-positioned"

SQ-0892's third note settles the criterion, from the user: **"is there art beneath this text?"** — not "is this text pixel-positioned". A glyph in a terminal cell carries its own background, which occludes artwork beneath it; that is a physical fact and not something to engineer around.

| beneath the text | correct treatment |
|---|---|
| the machine's plain ground | glyphs — the runs account for every pixel |
| artwork (Zork Zero's ribbon) | raster — no glyph can reproduce what is under it |

`over_art` (`screen.rs`, via `region_has_opaque`) is the existing predicate and already decides this correctly for chrome. The work is applying it to the story region and rasterising only where it answers yes — NOT rasterising the whole viewport whenever any art is present.

## What to be careful of

- **Do not rasterise more than the art demands.** SQ-0750 is the standard: *"reserve raster for pixels the runs cannot account for"*. A story region with a picture in one corner should keep crisp glyphs everywhere else. The failure mode is a frame that reads as "raster mode by accident".
- **Scrolling.** The chrome ring is static per frame; the story region scrolls. An art strip inside the viewport has to decide whether it scrolls with the prose (a margin float — SQ-0888's ship does this) or is pinned to the window (a backdrop). Both exist in the corpus and they are not the same mechanism.
- **The transcript already has an inline-image path** (`transcript_images`, margin floats, `ImageAlign::MarginLeft/Right`). Check whether the answer is to route more through it rather than to build a second mechanism beside it. SQ-0888 routed Shogun's Apple-press ship into the text stream precisely because pinned placement was the wrong shape.
- **Measure `story_clear_native` again afterwards.** SQ-0894 measured it as a no-op across the corpus (56 frames, zero differences) — but only over frames that REACH the ring. If this quest lets frames with art behind the prose reach it, the inset finally has something to find, and step (b) of the user's ordering becomes live for real.

## Acceptance

The four `picture_takeover` hatches have somewhere to send their pixels. That does not mean removing them — SQ-0897 owns that, one at a time by measurement.

Verify on the frames that motivate it: Zork Zero's `map` command (window 0 grows to the full screen with the map painted beneath — the `art_fills_screen` case, documented at `screen.rs:3868-3880`), fmvpoker's bet panel (`story_plate_escapes_story_window`, SQ-0739), and Shogun's Amiga credits screen (`James Clavell's Shogun.adf`, r295 — window 0 spans all 640px at the menu).

## What shipped

Three commits: dde4b127 (primitive + instrument), 80309a81 (wired into the ring), 3fd9ccda (spec addendum, `Confirm:`).

**The gap, located exactly.** It was not "hybrid cannot rasterise inside the viewport". It was that hybrid's viewport was the RAW win0 box, so art the game painted inside window 0 was in neither half of the screen: `classify_windows` sets a `win == 0` Graphics aside as `story_gfx` so the chrome canvas never carries it, and `blit_story_gfx` was reachable from the RASTER path alone.

**The fix builds no second mechanism, and that was the key decision.** The ring is `pane − viewport`. Move the viewport off the art and the art is INSIDE the ring, drawn by machinery unchanged since SQ-0505. Three edits:

1. `v6_layout::story_text_native(story, frame_art, story_gfx)` — `story_clear_native` past the frame art, then `story_prose_box` for what window 0's own plate leaves. `v6_layout::native_viewport_box` quantizes it; `story_viewport_box` now delegates to that and is unchanged.
2. `screen.rs` ring: TWO art canvases. `frame_art` (chrome only) is the inset's oracle; `gfx` (= frame_art + plate) is what every downstream stage asks "is there art here?" of. The plate is also blitted into `canvas`, the band pixel source, with the chrome ART layer (before `blit_paint_ground`, before window pages).
3. `story_prose_box == None` → plate owns the screen → viewport `Rect::new(area.x, area.y, area.width, 0)`, whole pane to the ring, `render_node` skipped and metrics `None` (raster's own answer on the same frame, SQ-0707).

Everything meaning "the box the viewport was cut from" now reads the derived rect `vp_native`: the SQ-0747 remainder walk, `ChromeRowOracle`'s `below_or_above` (its `story: &PositionedWindow` field became `story_native: (u32,u32,u32,u32)`), the chrome-text overlay's in-box test and row packing, and the `/dump-windows` viewport line. `story` still means the window.

## The judgment call I made alone

**The frame-art inset is ADVISORY; the plate is AUTHORITATIVE.** An inset leaving less than one 8x16 text cell is discarded and the window keeps its declared box. Without that floor, `hybrid_v6_model` (a solid opaque 320x200 chrome window with win0 at (43,39,234,160)) insets to `(122,119,76,0)` and takes a story region hybrid has drawn prose into since Lane H — three existing render tests failed on it. The floor is raster's own (`w >= 8 && h >= 16`, `screen.rs` applies it to `story_clear_native`). Consequence: a full-screen chrome BACKDROP under a full-screen story window gets no ring, which is precisely why `art_fills_screen` had to be KEPT in SQ-0897.

## Answers to the quest's four "careful of" items

- **Do not over-rasterise.** `story_prose_box` is a largest-free-rectangle sweep, so a picture in one corner costs the prose that corner and nothing else. The test asserts the untouched edges are byte-identical between the plate and no-plate runs.
- **Scrolling.** The plate is a BACKDROP pinned to the window — the game draws it at absolute native coordinates and it does not move with the prose. It goes to the ring, which is static per frame. Correct by construction.
- **`transcript_images`.** NOT the answer, and untouched. That path is for pictures the story printed into the TEXT STREAM (drop-caps, margin floats, SQ-0888's Apple ship). A pinned backdrop is a different object; routing it through the float path would make it scroll.
- **Measure `story_clear_native` again.** Done, and it is STILL a no-op on the corpus (`ring_scout --all --size 98x37`: every viewport byte-identical). The thing that was not a no-op is the PLATE half, which `story_clear_native` structurally cannot see — a centred plate touches no edge, and fmvpoker's hollow table touches all four and insets to width 0.

## Measurements (reproduce with `cargo run -q -p app --example ring_scout -- --all --no-tap --turns 8 --size 98x37`)

## Proof and falsification

No corpus frame exercises the new path yet, so a green gate proves nothing (SQ-0894's trap). The proof is a frame that reaches the ring with NO routing change: `plate_in_story_window_model` in `screen.rs`'s tests — a chrome FRAME (opaque 20px border, hollow middle) with window 0 at (40,40,240,120), inset from every screen edge so no `picture_takeover` arm fires, and a `win == 0` plate inside it. A/B on one frame, plate vs no plate, on the LEFT edge (a flank) and the TOP edge (a full-width strip), both `honor_game_colours` modes.

FALSIFIED, verbatim:
- viewport from the declared box instead of `story_text_native`: `honor=true: the story viewport starts at the plate's right edge, not the window's left edge — the text region is what the art LEAVES (SQ-0896). bare (5, 34) vs plated (5, 34)`.
- `blit_story_gfx` off the band canvas: `honor=true Top: the story window's own plate is drawn by the ring.` — and the LEFT case SURVIVES that revert, because a flank composes its source from the art canvas while a full-width strip crops the band canvas. That asymmetry is why both orientations are in the test; a left-only test would have passed against half a fix.
