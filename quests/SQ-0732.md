Fixture: `stories/THE BAT.gblorb.blorb` (Glulx). User's report: rooms are OVER-detected at startup — the map gains nodes that are not rooms before play begins.

THE MECHANISM, for whoever picks this up. Glulx room detection lives in `crates/app/src/glulx_session.rs` (~line 626): each turn it calls `self.appglk().take_room_heading()`, which surfaces the Inform 7 **Subheader**-styled line from the transcript, classifies the turn as Unchanged / Ambiguous / Changed, folds that into the `glulx_roomlock` learner, and reports the result as `LocationMethod::RoomHeading` — a method documented as "trusted directly — not subject to the NameOnly-empty-graph gate". That deliberate lack of a gate is the likely reason boot noise reaches the map.

PRIME SUSPECT, unmeasured: at startup an Inform 7 game prints its banner, title, author, release line, and often a menu or content warning — and any of those can carry the Subheader style that `take_room_heading` keys on. Every such line then looks exactly like walking into a room. FIRST MEASUREMENT: drive the boot sequence headlessly and log every heading `take_room_heading` returns before the first prompt, alongside what the game actually printed. `crates/app/tests/glulx_room_detection.rs` and `glulx_boot_room_id.rs` are the existing harnesses to model on.

WHY THIS IS PAIRED WITH SQ-0731, and why they should be one piece of work: Facility.z8 is the same Inform 7 heading convention arriving through the Z-machine, where detection never fires at all because the whole v4+ ladder is gated on a status line the game leaves empty. SQ-0731's obvious fix is to extend this Glulx mechanism to Z-machine games — but extending a mechanism that already over-fires would spread the bug to a second engine. Tighten the validation here FIRST, then extend the tightened version.

Whatever validation is added must leave the Glulx corpus detecting exactly as it does now — `glulx_maze_identity.rs` in particular pins the hard case, where the same heading is printed from different rooms and the room id has to come from the RAM global rather than the name.
