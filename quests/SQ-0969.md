Dispatched as a worktree lane together with SQ-0965 (both live in the Memory view: crates/app/src/debug_panel.rs, src/render/debug_panel.rs, src/session.rs memory_hex at :4865, src/engine.rs Debugger at :526).

Constraints given to the lane:
- Anchor the decode. Three Z-chars per 2-byte word (0x8000 terminates), and decode.rs:50 carries `alphabet`/`abbrev_pending` across words — Z-chars 4/5 shift the NEXT char's alphabet, 1/2/3 make the next an abbreviation index, shift-6 in A2 eats the next two for a 10-bit ZSCII escape. So an unanchored row decodes to wrong-but-plausible text. Rows with no known string start keep the ASCII column; never show a decode you cannot vouch for.
- Abbreviations expand to arbitrary length, so a row can exceed 24 chars — the column is variable-width.
- Keep the ASCII column reachable (both columns, or a toggle).
- Keep the `debug.zstring` theme selector alive and pointed at the new column if the caption goes.
- Must degrade to no column for Glulx/Scott without special-casing at the render site.
