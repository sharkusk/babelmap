EVIDENCE GATHERED (2026-07-28), not yet fixed — do not guess further without reproducing.

Symptom (user, two screenshots): under Journey's left image frame a black bar appears at some
pane widths and not others, and its height varies with WIDTH. When it is absent, "the story
window and left frame are equal in size".

Headless sweep, Journey at the command menu, hybrid, REAL cell metrics
(Picker::from_fontsize(8x18) — NOT halfblocks, whose 1x2 cells put the layout in a completely
different regime and never reproduce this):

  w=96   vp h33 menu@35 → rows 31,32 L=fill(34,34,34) R=Reset | row 33 L=R=65,65,65  | 34,35 L=R=Black
  w=116  vp h32 menu@33 → rows 30,31 L=fill          R=Reset | (no band row at all)  | 32,33 L=R=Black
  w=138  vp h30 menu@32 → rows 28,29 L=fill          R=Reset | row 30 L=R=139,139,139| 31,32 L=R=Black

So: a 1–2 row strip sits BETWEEN the flank band's bottom (viewport.bottom()) and the menu strip
top, and its size/content changes with width — sometimes a light band row, sometimes nothing.
That strip is where the black bar lives.

Why the current code leaves it: `menu_flank_panel`'s fill covers only the flank BAND rect, which
`chrome_bands(area, viewport)` sizes to the viewport's rows. `flank_divider_extension` likewise
stops at `menu_top_row` passed as `viewport.bottom()`. Neither covers rows below viewport.bottom()
but above the actual menu strip — a gap that only opens up at widths where the menu's
bottom-anchored scale rounds its top row below the viewport's bottom.

LIKELY FIX (unverified): extend the flank fill (and probably the divider extension) down to the
real menu strip top rather than viewport.bottom(); the menu band's own `y` is available via
`menu_bands`/`menu_strips` at the call site in screen.rs. Verify with the sweep above at 8x18
metrics — halfblocks will NOT show it.

CAUTION: my probes at halfblocks metrics reported "no suspicious rows" at every width and were
misleading. Always reproduce this one with from_fontsize(8,18).
