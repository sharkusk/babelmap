DONE, and the report was half wrong in a useful way. **The art is not off-centre.** Measured on `journey-r83-s890706.z6` at 98x37 (784x666 device): the lock floors s 1.225 → 1.0, `off_x = 72`, the 640px screen covers device 72..712, and a sweep of 80–160 columns on the `.adf` found `gapL == gapR` at every width with the lock on.

**The letterbox GROUND is off-centre**, which is what the eye saw. At that same frame the left margin carried 333 cells of the picture panel's own `Rgb(34,34,34)` against 54 cells of bare `Reset` on the right. Two hybrid-ring cell fills were bounded by the BAND, and a band runs to the pane edge: `draw_chrome_text_strip`'s strip and per-row bar floods, and `menu_flank_panel`'s panel fill, whose `(lo, hi)` falls back to `band.x`/`band.right()` when a border column is not found — which is r83 exactly, since its outer rule is a reverse-video SPACE that the glyph-border probe does not return. Hence left-only spill, hence the asymmetry.

Fixed with one shared helper, `v6_layout::screen_cols(scale, native_w, cell_px, pane)`, quantized OUTWARD to match `edge_glyph_col` rather than the viewport's inward rounding; both fills clip to it. Not lock-specific in principle — a height-bound pane opens the same margin while free-scaling.

Suite `v6_pixel_lock_centring.rs`: 5 titles x 4 panes x both `honor_game_colours`, oracle is margin AGREEMENT rather than emptiness (a story page floods the pane deliberately, SQ-0532). Falsified — with either old bound restored it fails with `left: ["Black", "Rgb(34, 34, 34)"] / right: ["Black", "Reset"]`. The arithmetic case PASSES against the unfixed code, which is the measurement proving the centring was never at fault. Zork Zero r393, Arthur r74 and Shogun r322 were symmetric before and after.

Landed as `04b4cc2b`, merged in `9ff274cc`.
