## Measured: the hint screen is ONE screen, shared by at least two titles

Probed Zork Zero (Blorb r393, Amiga r366, Mac r296) and Shogun (Blorb r322, Amiga r295) by booting each medium through startup.rs's full chain, driving `hint` then `y`, and dumping the window list, win7's alpha coverage by region, and every run's position.

### The frame artwork is identical, and it is a ring

win7's opaque coverage on the hint screen, EVERY medium of BOTH titles:

    top band (y<80)   78%
    left flank (x<86) 70%
    right flank       70%
    MIDDLE             0%
    whole canvas    30.5%

Zork Zero gameplay is 30.1–30.3% with top 88% / flanks 58%; Shogun gameplay is
12.1–14.4% with top 14% / flanks 43–53%. So the games REDRAW the border for the
hint screen and both land on the same 30.5% ring. The middle is 0% opaque on
every frame measured — that is what makes it a ring, and what makes the middle
text glyph territory.

### The text lands in the same two slots, with the same strings

Shogun r322 hint frame:
    GRID 500x70  @ 70,0    6 runs: y=1 " InvisiClues (tm)", y=17 "N for next item.",
                                    y=33 "P for previous item.", y=33 "(Or use mouse.)"
    GRID 500x330 @ 70,70  22 runs: the topic list, y 71..375

Zork Zero r393 hint frame:
    GRID 640x78  @ 0,0     6 runs: THE SAME FOUR STRINGS at the same y (1, 17, 33)
                                    and the same title x (248)
    GRID 468x322 @ 86,78  22 runs: the topic list, y 79..383

Same InvisiClues subsystem, same layout, different middle rect. This is not two
similar screens, it is one screen.

### The only variable is what carries the middle text

    Zork Zero PC r393    middle = GRID 468x322 @ 86,78   buffer WITHDRAWN
    Zork Zero Amiga r366 middle = GRID 464x320 @ 88,80   buffer WITHDRAWN
    Shogun PC r322       middle = GRID 500x330 @ 70,70   buffer WITHDRAWN
    Zork Zero Mac r296   middle = full-screen GRID 640x400, buffer KEPT

A withdrawn buffer makes `layout.story` None, so routing falls to the SQ-0477
runs-only arm, whose contract is "this path draws the RUNS and nothing else --
every pixel on the screen is discarded". That is what throws the ring away. The
Mac keeps its buffer, reaches the ring, and keeps the artwork — which is why the
user called it the most nearly correct of the three.

### Rendered, to confirm rather than infer

`render_story_pane` at 100x34, searching the pane for the menu's own words:

    Zork Zero PC    hybrid  path="painted (hint/menu takeover)"  all 4 words, NO artwork
    Zork Zero Amiga hybrid  path="painted (hint/menu takeover)"  all 4 words, NO artwork
    Zork Zero Mac   hybrid  path="hybrid-ring"                   artwork kept, 2 of 4 words

CAVEAT ON THAT LAST ROW: the search is over CELLS, so it cannot see chrome text
that was rasterised into the band image. The Mac's missing title may be present
as pixels. Settling it needs the pty/oracle layer, not the cell harness.

## Where the band text belongs (user's correction, 2026-08-20)

The banner title and key legend sit ON the artwork, so they must be RASTER text
— a terminal glyph cannot sit over a kitty placement. This is NOT Shogun's boot
menu, whose items sat on the machine's own ground between the panels and could
be glyphs (SQ-0886/SQ-0892). What IS like Shogun's boot menu is the topic list in
the clear middle.

**The ring already does exactly this and needs no new mechanism.** `glyph_rows`
is built from `ChromeStrip::Text` strips only; `ChromeStrip::Art` contributes
nothing, so an art strip keeps its rasterised text while a pure-text strip is
drawn with glyphs (SQ-0903 feeds that decision to `build_chrome_canvas` before it
paints). Shogun proves the classification has to stay dynamic rather than
per-window: its top band is 14% opaque during gameplay and 78% on the hint
screen, so the same window slot is a text strip in one frame and art in the next.

## Proposed fix (NOT yet approved)

Detect the story surface as "the window filling the ring's clear middle",
Buffer or Grid, instead of "a primary Buffer". `layout.story` then becomes Some
on every medium of both titles and the existing ring path handles the rest —
bands from content (SQ-0894), banner text rasterised because its strip is art,
topic list packed into the viewport as glyphs (SQ-0892).

Probe lives at crates/app/examples/scratch_hint_probe.rs (untracked scratch).

--- note 2026-08-20T15:05:43Z ---

User, 2026-08-20, on seeing the promoted grid rasterised behind its own glyphs: "i thought we had controls in place to not rasterize any text that is using terminal text".

We do, and this is exactly why the defect is a gap rather than a missing feature. The control is `TextLayer::SkipGlyphRows(&glyph_rows)` reaching `build_chrome_canvas` before it paints (SQ-0903), and `glyph_rows` is built from `text_run_tops` — which collects runs from `ChromeStrip::Text` strips ONLY, with `ChromeStrip::Art` contributing none.

That is the right rule for the ring's bands: a strip whose pixels the runs fully explain is drawn with glyphs, a strip with artwork under the text keeps its rasterised text (a glyph cannot sit over a kitty placement).

The promoted middle grid is neither. It is not a band, so it is not a strip, so no strip classification ever sees it and its rows never enter `glyph_rows` — while the in-box packing (SQ-0892) draws it as glyphs anyway. Hence both, one on top of the other.

So the fix is to bring the promoted grid under the existing control rather than to invent one: either its rows join `glyph_rows`, or it is filtered out of the chrome slice handed to `build_chrome_canvas` (it is the story surface for that frame, not chrome art). The second is closer to what it means.

Filed alongside, from the same session: SQ-0935 (DOS Shogun's blue page only appears after the hint menu round-trip) and SQ-0936 (locked integer/half-step v6 scaling, centred).

## The raster leak, found only once the capture went through the Ghostty path

The cell-buffer harness said `art_inside=0` and was wrong to be trusted: it renders
through HALFBLOCKS, where an image is painted into cells, while the user runs kitty,
where an image is a placement that covers them. CLAUDE.md names this exact blind
spot. Every measurement before the escalation was of the layer that cannot answer
the question. The user had to say "you should be generating the frame using the
ghostty path!" before it was used.

### What the user's screenshot showed (114x50, Amiga press)

  - a clipped one-character-wide column of text down the inner edge of the panel,
    including a second highlighted PROLOGUE
  - the reverse-video selection bar's two ENDS appearing inside the left and right
    flanks, level with the selected item and OVERRUNNING the frame
  - the same pair repeated lower down — "mirrored top/bottom"

### Cause

`build_chrome_canvas` was still being handed the promoted story grid. It is in
`chrome` because its runs must reach `chrome_runs`, but it is the STORY SURFACE,
and rasterising a story surface is the thing hybrid exists not to do. So the menu's
pixels — its text and the selection bar — went into the canvas the ring bands are
cropped from, and came back out inside the ring. The "mirror" is not a mirror: it is
a flank band TILING (SQ-0894), repeating a strip that should never have carried text
in the first place.

Two earlier partial fixes were on the way to this and neither was sufficient alone:
`render_node` drawing the grid while the in-box packing stamped it again (fixed by
skipping the promoted grid in the packing — all three Zork Zero presses then agreed
at 227 glyph cells where two had been inflated), and the page flood, which
`story_bg_rgba` now supplies for a Grid.

### Fix

Build the ring's canvas and fill its window pages from a chrome list with the
promoted story grid filtered out. The middle is drawn as terminal cells by
`render_node`, which is the point of hybrid; the canvas only needs what the bands
ship, and the bands are the frame.

### Verified

`cargo run -p app --example pty_capture` at 114x50 on the Amiga press, selection
driven down to CRAG & ENVIRONS: no stray column, selection bar contained to its own
item, no repeat in the flanks. The oracle reports "rows 8..47 are PAINTED CELLS
(SGR background spans), NOT a placement rect: no kitty placement covers them".
