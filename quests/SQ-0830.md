--- note 2026-08-13T07:45:02Z ---

Measured on `Journey - The Quest Begins.adf` (release 30, serial 890322), hybrid, kitty, cell 8x18.

`hybrid_bottom_plan` opens with `if slack == 0 { return BottomPlan::Letterbox; }` — before `menu_strip_below_story` is ever consulted. So on any pane whose VERTICAL axis is the binding letterbox axis, Journey's command menu stops being recognised as a menu and the plan is `Letterbox`.

Everything gated on `plan == Menu` switches off at once:
  * `flank_panels` is empty, so `menu_flank_panel` never runs — no panel fill sampled from the art's own edge, no vertical centring, and (since SQ-0828) no aspect-correct dest box;
  * `tiled_flanks` is no longer excluded, so the picture column falls through `v6_border::recognize` to the side-border TILER — the path SQ-0819 established is wrong for Journey, whose left column is a picture seated in a panel, not a border to extend;
  * `glyph_borders_only` flips true.

Plan and arm, across the twelve-pane sweep:
   60x24 menu/panel · 80x24 menu/panel · 100x30 menu/panel · 117x41 menu/panel ·
   120x30 LETTERBOX/tiled · 130x50 menu/panel · 140x40 menu/panel · 150x60 menu/panel ·
   164x41 LETTERBOX/tiled · 180x50 LETTERBOX/tiled · 200x60 menu/panel · 236x65 LETTERBOX/tiled

**The user's own 166x44 is one of them.** The v6 pane is 164x41 cells = 1312x738 device px, s = min(1312/640, 738/400) = 1.845 exactly, so the vertical slack is 0. The plate is then drawn by `draw_chrome_band_image` from a 247x283 crop at the uniform scale, where its magnification cannot be chosen independently at all — which is also why SQ-0824's integer snap is unavailable at exactly the size the defect was reported at.

SQ-0819's specific catastrophe is NOT reproducing: "The Party" and "Proceed" are both present in the rendered buffer at 164x41 and 180x50. This is the picture column losing its panel treatment, and losing it as a function of the pane's aspect ratio rather than of anything about the game.

Likely also the layout difference reported against v0.1.0-beta.5, where the same capture transmitted the art at 560x486 into a 70x27 placement plus a separate 88x486 flank band, against HEAD's 456x522 into 57x29 with no flank.

Probable fix: ask `menu_strip_below_story` BEFORE the `slack == 0` shortcut, or keep the Menu classification independent of slack and let the reclaim be the thing slack gates. That is a plan-selection change with blast radius across Zork Zero / Shogun / Arthur, so it wants its own lane.
